# Supported movements

Catchline analyses:

- **Snatch**
- **Clean**
- **Clean & jerk**: film the clean and the jerk in one clip. The jerk is analysed after its clean; a jerk filmed on its own is not analysed yet.
- **Bar muscle-up**

Coming next: **butterfly pull-up**. Toes-to-bar, chest-to-bar and ring muscle-up are on the list after that.

Pick the movement before you analyse. For a clip shared into Catchline, it suggests the movement ("Looks like a snatch. Right?") and you can change it.
