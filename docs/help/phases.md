# What each phase means

Catchline splits each rep into phases and shows the numbers it measured in each one. Step through them in the breakdown with the arrows either side of the video.

## Snatch and clean

- **Setup**: you and the bar before it leaves the floor.
- **1st pull**: the bar leaves the floor and travels to the knee.
- **Transition**: the bar passes the knee and the hips come forward.
- **2nd pull**: the drive up to full extension.
- **Turnover**: you pull under and turn the bar over.
- **Catch**: you receive the bar, overhead in the snatch or on the shoulders in the clean.
- **Recovery**: you stand up with it.

From the hang there is a **Hang** phase in place of the 1st pull and transition: the bar lowers to your start height, then the 2nd pull follows.

## Jerk (after a clean)

- **Setup**: the bar on your shoulders.
- **Drive**: the dip and drive up.
- **Turnover**: you move under the bar.
- **Catch**: the bar locked out overhead.
- **Recovery**: you bring your feet back and stand.

## Bar muscle-up

- **Arch**: the swing behind the bar.
- **Hollow**: the swing in front.
- **Hip drive**: the hips open and drive you up to the bar.
- **Recovery**: on top of the bar and back down.

The phases and the cue are an AI estimate from the clip, not a coach's judgement.
