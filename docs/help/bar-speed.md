# Bar speed explained

**Peak bar speed** is the fastest the bar travels upwards during the lift, from leaving the floor to the catch, in metres per second (m/s).

Catchline works it out from the bar path and the size of the plate in the clip, so it needs:

- the near plate clearly in view, to set the scale
- a steady phone and a steady bar track

When either is missing, bar speed shows as **not seen** rather than a guess. That happens when the scale had to come from a partly seen plate or from body proportions, or when the track was too unsteady to time.

Compare bar speed between your own lifts filmed the same way. A different angle or distance changes the reading.
