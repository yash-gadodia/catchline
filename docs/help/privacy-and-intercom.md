# Privacy: what stays on your phone and what Intercom sees

## Your video never leaves your iPhone

Catchline analyses the lift on your phone. The video is never uploaded and never used to train AI. We measure positions, not faces.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your original clips.** You choose how long Catchline keeps its copies under You, Auto-delete originals.
- **Body positions and bar path**, as numbers, not video, so your history improves when the analysis does. They stay on your phone and in your own iCloud backup, never on our servers.
- **Five stills per lift**, for the strobe and the evidence crop.

## What reaches our servers

A short record of each lift, so your history and your free count follow your account: the movement, the weight, made or missed, the cue text, why there was no cue, the start position, a rough camera angle, the analysis version, any failure code, whether it retested a cue, and the time. No video, stills, positions or bar path.

## What Intercom sees

We use Intercom for chat and help. Intercom sees:

- what you write to us
- the app version, update id, phone model and your plan
- your account id, if you signed in with Apple
- events with labels only: an analysis finished or failed (with the movement, the error code, made or missed, and whether a cue was shown), the paywall was shown, a trial is ending, your fifth analysis, the subscription manager was opened

Intercom never sees your video, stills, body positions or bar path.

## Only if you tick the box

When you report a wrong result, you can tick **Attach analysis numbers (no video)**. It is off by default. Ticked, your report carries a short summary: the movement, phase times, made or missed, the angle, the confidence, the measured values and any error code. You see exactly what is attached before you send.
