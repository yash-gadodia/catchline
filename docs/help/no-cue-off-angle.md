# Why there is no cue, or the cue says "filmed at an angle"

Catchline gives one cue only when it trusts the numbers behind it. When it does not, you still get the bar path and the phases, and the screen says **No cue this time** with the reason and how to film it next time.

## The reasons

- **Filmed off side-on.** Forward and back distances are not reliable from an angle. Film from the side, phone level with the bar.
- **The plate was partly hidden.** The lift could not be sized. Keep the near plate fully in view, nothing in front of it.
- **Part of the rep is outside the clip.** Start filming before you set up and stop once you stand.
- **The rep was missed, and nothing measured explains why.** Film one more rep, or a lighter make, to compare.
- **The measurements were too close to call.** Film from the side, phone level with the bar.
- **The bar was lost overhead.** Nothing past the pull can be judged. Film from the side with the whole bar in frame overhead, and keep the phone still.

## A cue marked "filmed at an angle"

Sometimes Catchline shows a cue but rates it medium or low confidence, for example "Confidence: low · filmed at an angle". The cue is worth checking, not certain. Film one more set side on to confirm it.

## Nothing to fix

**Clean rep** means the lift was measured and nothing needed fixing. That is different from no cue.

## Ask why

On a no-cue or off-angle result, tap **Ask why** to message us. The message starts with the angle, the confidence and the lift, so you only need to add your question.
