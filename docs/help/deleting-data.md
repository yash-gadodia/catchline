# Deleting your lifts and your account

## One lift

In **Lifts**, press and hold the lift, then tap **Delete**. Its cue, numbers and stills go too. Your free video count stays the same.

## Original clips

Under **You**, **Auto-delete originals** sets how long Catchline keeps its copies of your clips, or Never. When your phone runs low on space, Catchline offers to delete the originals; your analyses, cues and stills stay either way.

## Your account

Under **You**, tap **Delete account** and type the word shown to confirm. Every lift, cue and still is removed within 24 hours, and Catchline starts fresh on this phone. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

## Your conversations with us

Messages you sent through chat are kept in Intercom. Ask us in the chat and we will delete them.
