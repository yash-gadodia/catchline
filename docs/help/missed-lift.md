# What "missed lift" means

Catchline marks a rep as missed when the bar comes down instead of being held overhead or in the rack.

On a missed rep you may see:

- **Missed lift: the bar came down in front.** or **Missed lift: the bar came down behind.** This is where the bar went, not why.
- **You missed because** followed by the cue, when a measured fault explains the miss.

If the rep was missed and nothing measured explains it, there is no cue. Film one more rep, or a lighter make, so there is something to compare with.

The path, the phases and the numbers before the miss are still measured.
