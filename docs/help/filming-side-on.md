# How to film a lift

Catchline measures the lift from the side. A good clip is most of a good result.

## Barbell lifts (snatch, clean, clean & jerk)

- Film side on: the camera square to your side, level with the bar.
- Get your whole body and the bar in frame, including the bar overhead.
- Keep the phone still. Stand it on the floor or on a stack of plates.
- Stand it three to four metres away. Further than that and small drifts are lost.
- Keep the near plate fully in view, with nothing in front of it. Catchline uses the plate to size the lift.

## Bar muscle-up

- Film side on to the rig.
- Get the bar, your hands and your feet in frame.
- Keep the phone still at hip height, far enough back to see the whole swing.

## Every clip

- One set per clip. Catchline finds the reps.
- Start filming before you set up and stop once you are standing.
- Face a light. If the plates and the floor are the same shade, the bar is hard to see.
- Film in Catchline, or set your camera to 60 fps. At 24 fps the path is drawn but the phases cannot be timed.

If a clip cannot be read, Catchline says why on the result screen and keeps the clip so you can try again.
