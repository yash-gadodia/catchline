# Plans, trials and restoring a purchase

## Plans

- **Free**: 3 videos, for life.
- **Pull**: $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year. Unlimited analysis (a fair-use limit of 1,000 a month guards against abuse).
- **Founding member**: $29.99 once, Pull for life with no renewal. Limited to 500 seats.

Prices are shown in US dollars here; the App Store shows your local price before you pay.

## Free trial

Some offers start with a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged on the day you start. The first charge is on the date shown, unless you cancel, and we remind you two days before.

## Cancelling

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Catchline. In the app, a paid plan's **Change plan** button (You, Plan) opens the subscription manager. Deleting your Catchline account does not cancel a subscription.

## Restoring a purchase

On a new phone, or after reinstalling, tap **Restore** on the paywall. Catchline checks your Apple ID and brings your plan back. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

## Still stuck?

Tap **Purchase problem?** under Restore on the paywall. The message starts with your plan, so we can look it up.
