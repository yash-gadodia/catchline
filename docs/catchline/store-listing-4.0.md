### Description

```
Add a video of your lift. Get it back with the bar path, your body lines and your joint angles drawn on the real video.

WORKS ON
Snatch, clean and jerk, with hang, power and split variations. Squat and deadlift. Pull-ups, muscle-ups and other gymnastics movements.

WHAT YOU SEE
- The bar path over the lift
- A vertical start line and a floor line
- Body lines and joint angles on every frame
- Frame-by-frame stepping and slow motion
- Your own lines and angles, drawn on any frame
- A video to share, with the lines burned in
Turn any overlay on or off.

PRIVATE
Tracking runs on your iPhone. Your video never leaves it and is never used for training.

GOOD TO KNOW
Lines and angles are estimates from video. For the bar path, film from the side with the whole plate in frame; when the plate is not clear, the path follows your hands and the app says so. No rep counts, no bar speed.

PLANS
Free: 3 videos a week, with every overlay.
Catchline Pro: unlimited videos, $2.99/month or $19.99/year.

Prices in USD, vary by country. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage them in your App Store account settings.

Terms: https://yash-gadodia.github.io/catchline/legal/terms.html
Privacy: https://yash-gadodia.github.io/catchline/legal/privacy.html
```

### App Review notes

```
Hello App Review team,

Version 4.0 simplifies Catchline. It no longer gives technique cues. It tracks a short video of a weightlifting or gymnastics movement and plays it back with the bar path, body lines and joint angles drawn on the video.

HOW TO TEST
No account is needed. On first launch: Get started, Next, then Choose a video (any video of a person lifting a barbell or doing pull-ups) or Film a lift. Trim it and tap Track. Tracking runs on the device and takes roughly as long as the clip on a recent iPhone, longer on older ones. The result opens in a player: play, step frame by frame, switch the path, lines, body and angles on and off, draw on a frame, and Share to export a video or image with the overlays.

CAMERA, PHOTO LIBRARY AND PRIVACY
The camera films the user's own lift. Users can also pick an existing video; iOS may ask for library access so that videos stored only in iCloud can be opened, and declining still works for videos on the device. The video is processed entirely on the iPhone with bundled Core ML models and is never uploaded. The app stores the trimmed clip, the tracked positions and two still frames in its own folder on the phone. No face recognition. Microphone access is not requested. When online, a small record per video (date, optional load, whether tracking finished) syncs to our server so purchases and history follow the account.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Free use: 3 tracked videos per calendar week. The paywall appears when the user starts a 4th video in a week, and from You > Plan.
Catchline Pro (subscription group "Catchline"): $2.99/month or $19.99/year, unlimited videos. The paywall also offers the existing non-consumable "Founding member" lifetime purchase. Earlier tiers (Hook, Platform) are no longer offered in the app; customers who own any earlier tier keep unlimited use. Restore Purchases is on the paywall. Sign in with Apple is asked for before a purchase so the plan belongs to an account.

ACCOUNT DELETION
You > Delete account. The user types DELETE to confirm.

The app makes no medical claims. Lines and angles are labelled as estimates.

Thank you. Contact: [SUPPORT_EMAIL], [PHONE]
```
