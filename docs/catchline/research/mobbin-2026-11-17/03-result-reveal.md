# 03. Result reveal

Mobbin study, 17 November 2026, iOS only. Adds to the October notes (study 27, sections 1.6, 1.11 and 1.18), which already cover The Outsiders' band-and-sentence layout, the Bevel paragraph, Tonal's summary, Nike Run Club's flat badge and the Noom, DoorDash, Chime, Onfido and Starling failure screens.

**The most useful thing:** Hevy's first-workout screen is the model. One line ("This is your 1st workout"), the result already laid out as a share card with three numbers, the share targets in a row under it, and a single "Done". The detailed screen comes after.
Catchline opens on the dense player. It should open on the clip playing once with the path drawn, one number, and Share, Save and "See the detail", with the twelve controls one tap away.

## Patterns

### 1. The reveal is the share card

The first thing shown after the work is the result in the shape it would be posted in, with the share targets directly under it.

- Hevy, first workout: "Nice work!", "This is your 1st workout", a photo card with "Duration 1h 5min", "Volume 1,740 kg", "Sets 17" and the handle. Under it "Share workout - Tag @hevyapp" and a row: Background, Stories, More, Workout Link, Copy Text. One button, "Done". Light confetti. https://mobbin.com/screens/db9dd20e-1263-4da0-88ef-4fdca528cc6a
- pushr: a white card floating over the blurred app. "28 push-ups" at display size, date and time, then four small stats (total time, total rest, sets, average push-up duration). Share and Instagram icons under the card, nothing else. https://mobbin.com/screens/50a35ef8-8d21-455c-a411-cd941749d767
- Runbuds: a flat red card with three numbers in slanted white blocks ("2:49", "0.5 km", "6:15 / km"), the date, pager dots for other layouts, and three round targets: Share, Story, Messages. https://mobbin.com/screens/fe94dfd4-f381-45d1-9022-eb6c9b3fc813
- Future Pro: a tall card with "#2" in the corner, the coach's name, and three stacked numbers (3.73 Miles, 405 Est. Calorie Burn, 35:00 Duration). A camera button and a full-width "Share". https://mobbin.com/screens/dcc4e946-a776-4295-ba32-16cf358e2358

Why it works: the person sees their result as something finished and theirs before they see it as data. Sharing costs one tap because the card is already made. Future Pro's "#2" and Hevy's "1st" make the count part of the result.

### 2. One hero number, then a small line that compares it with the person's own history

The largest thing on the screen is one value. The comparison sits directly under it in small type.

- The Outsiders, workout detail: under "Key Stats" a caption, "vs 30-day Strength Training avg". Each stat ("Total Time 1:03:08", "Training Load 79", "Avg HR 133 bpm", "Energy 254 kcal") has a small chip under it with a down arrow and the average ("1:04:04", "105", "143 bpm", "307 kcal"). https://mobbin.com/screens/98638987-60b2-4189-bee0-cd4f04fea285
- Strava, run "Results": three counts across the top (Best Efforts 3, Segments 3, Achievements 6), then rows with a PR medal: "1K / 2:58 / New best of all-time!". https://mobbin.com/screens/97ab8653-80ea-4cd0-9145-fe05a4fd1d8b
- Fi (dog activity): "Comparison. Tilda's total activity time was up over 100% Wed, Feb 11 vs Tue, Feb 10." then an arrow in a circle and "1hr 6min" in large green type, then the two days as lines on one chart. https://mobbin.com/screens/ac29fa20-047e-40ec-804d-e9f063596987
- Withings Health Mate: "Congrats! Your score increased by 1 point this week." above "61 out of 100". https://mobbin.com/screens/3defe666-ccc4-4f4c-b7c8-9f648bb736c3

Why it works: a number alone needs knowledge to read. "vs your 30-day average" needs none. Fi writes the comparison as a sentence first and shows the number second, which suits a person who is not yet fluent in the metric.

### 3. The first result of its kind is named as the first

When there is no history to compare with, the screen says so and explains the number once.

- Oura: "73 GOOD", headline "Your first Sleep Score!", then a paragraph: "Your Sleep Score sums up how well you've slept and looks at your previous night's sleep quality in relation to general sleep recommendations for your age group..." with "Show more". Contributors follow below. https://mobbin.com/screens/ded88fa6-85b6-460f-89b9-9eb71fcd2ef9
- Hevy: "This is your 1st workout" (above).
- Ladder: a metal coin with "1" on it, the workout name "WELCOME WORKOUT", a "Share Proof" button with the Instagram mark, then the totals (34:11 Total Time, 174 Total Calories, 135 Active Calories). https://mobbin.com/screens/f0e145f3-685c-4dfe-9ad3-ee080c0a6f82

Why it works: the first result has no trend, which is normally the interesting part. Saying "first" turns the missing comparison into an occasion and gives a reason to explain the metric that would be noise on the tenth.

### 4. A beat before the number

A short screen or state that announces the result, then the result.

- Cleo AI: a near-empty screen, "Score reveal time. You ready?", then the next screen: "63 out of 100" in a soft glow, a chip "Based on your answers", and a card "Your summary: You're on the right track, but there's room to improve". https://mobbin.com/screens/168cc234-04a7-45ae-bfd3-5b0b6eedb81c and https://mobbin.com/screens/8c905f3c-e573-42b4-a31e-faf44c7606f2
- Lemonade Insurance: "ANALYZING YOUR DATA..." resolves to "Analysis Complete" with a tick, four star-rated lines, then "Ok, we're all set. Want to see your price?" and the button "YEAH, LET'S SEE IT". https://mobbin.com/screens/cc8c461b-0bee-4d00-8a40-4fe4f9b4cd07
- Breathwrk: the raw measure first ("4.8 seconds"), then "Your Lung Health Score is" and a ring with 54. Two steps on one screen: what was measured, then what it means. https://mobbin.com/screens/daa63b01-b6a9-4ff1-a681-693e9f88cbb9

Why it works: a tap to reveal makes the result something the person chose to see, and it gives the app a clean moment to start an animation from. It must be one beat. Lemonade's is a single button.

### 5. Verdict word, one sentence, then the detail folded

Number, a one-word band, a short human sentence, and the breakdown behind a disclosure.

- Garmin Connect, sleep: "84 / 100 Score", then "Good Quality" and "6h 55m Duration", then a bold line "Shorter than ideal, restorative", two short sentences, and "Sleep Score Factors" as a collapsed row. https://mobbin.com/screens/6a79260c-a24f-40ef-be65-245841fd9a20
- Noom, face scan result: "Vascular elasticity / 1.47 s", a four-band scale (Needs attention, Fair, Good, Optimal) with the person's band filled and the ranges printed under each, then two paragraphs and "Done". https://mobbin.com/screens/c2f6a0f1-162b-4534-8aa1-ed7ad9f60a76
- Warby Parker: "Your face scan results" as three collapsed rows, each already showing its answer ("Face shape: Round", "Suggested frame widths: Wide, Medium"). One is expanded to show the explanation. Buttons "Continue" and "Rescan". https://mobbin.com/screens/5813f280-ce17-4ca0-9b44-dfcdf6187643
- Ahead: two numbers centred on a white screen and a text link, "Scroll down to see details", with a chevron. https://mobbin.com/screens/061ce41f-838f-469a-aec3-f1509357c2e2

Why it works: Warby Parker's rows carry the answer while closed, so the summary and the table of contents are the same thing. Noom's printed ranges let the person place themselves without trusting a colour.

### 6. Achievement shown as a banner on the normal result, not a takeover

The record is announced inside the ordinary result screen.

- Garmin Connect: a dismissible card at the top of the activity, "New Personal Record. Congratulations on achieving a new personal best!" with "View New Record". The map and stats sit under it unchanged. Tags "1 KM" and "LONGEST RUN" on the title. https://mobbin.com/screens/93d4a1e1-00a5-4562-bcd8-cb4b00d1a47e
- Strava: PR medals inline in the Results rows, and under them a card that turns the record into the next action: "PR today, ready for your next?". https://mobbin.com/screens/97ab8653-80ea-4cd0-9145-fe05a4fd1d8b
- adidas Running, record page: "MOST CALORIES BURNED", a badge, "257 cal", "Achieved on 12/2/24", and two square full-width buttons, "SHARE" and "CHECK ACTIVITY". Condensed capitals, black and white, no confetti. https://mobbin.com/screens/a7abb086-ec80-404f-85d3-284846b80ea7

Why it works: the person keeps their place. The adidas page is the closest visual match to Catchline's language found in this study: condensed display type, square buttons, one gold accent.

### 7. Failure names one cause, gives the fix, and keeps the retry in the same place

The failed state says which condition was not met and puts the retry where the result would have been.

- Fly Delta: "Passport Scan Unsuccessful. We were unable to scan your passport. For the best scan:" then two bullets specific to the failure ("Try to reduce movement", "Ensure all information at the bottom of your passport photo page is visible") and "Retry Scan". The general tips sheet is still visible behind. https://mobbin.com/screens/d230011e-a4a3-4daa-8ec1-08d9a38bbc52
- IKEA Kreativ: the failed scan stays as a card in the list. "Unable to process. Uh-oh. We were unable to process your capture. Learn how to capture your space better here." "Capture again" is the next card down. https://mobbin.com/screens/2ec2c58f-2df0-4ab3-b3df-1e50bc3213f8
- Warby Parker and Ulta Beauty put the redo on the success screen too: "Rescan" under "Continue", and "MATCH ME AGAIN" under "TRY ON". Ulta also asks "Do you like these results?" with thumbs. https://mobbin.com/screens/5813f280-ce17-4ca0-9b44-dfcdf6187643 and https://mobbin.com/screens/3acadcaa-5855-428b-88f9-e003fc8e5aa2
- Amazon Alexa: "Device not discovered. Here are a few tips:" as a numbered list of three, then "Try Again". https://mobbin.com/screens/a50fe432-f4cf-447a-8669-46546338f126

Why it works: Delta's two bullets are about this failure, not a general checklist. Offering a redo on a successful result admits that a result can be poor without being a failure, which is Catchline's case when the body tracks and the bar does not.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on the first lift, then on sharing. The figures in the copy below (1.84 m/s, 4 cm, 96%) are placeholders to show the layout, not measurements.

1. **Open on a reveal screen, not the player** (patterns 1 and 4). The reveal shows the clip full height, 9:16. It plays once at normal speed with the bar path drawing in red as the bar moves, and holds on the last frame with the whole path visible. No scrubber, no toggles. Under the clip: one number and three actions. The existing player opens from "SEE THE DETAIL".
2. **One hero number with its comparison** (pattern 2). Under the clip, in condensed display type: "1.84 M/S" and the label "PEAK BAR SPEED · AI ESTIMATE". Under that, small: "0.12 faster than your last clean" when there is a previous lift of the same movement. Follow The Outsiders: the comparison is a chip under the number, not a sentence beside it.
3. **Name the first one** (pattern 3). On the first tracked lift there is nothing to compare with, so the small line reads "Your first tracked lift" and a second line explains the picture once: "The red line is the path of the bar." From the second lift of a movement, the comparison replaces it.
4. **Share, Save and Detail directly under the number, at 56pt, square** (patterns 1 and 6). "SHARE" primary, "SAVE" and "SEE THE DETAIL" secondary, in the adidas layout of stacked full-width buttons. "TRACK ANOTHER" appears on the detail screen and in Lifts, not here, since on the free plan the next one may be a week away and the button would lead to the paywall at the moment of the wow.
5. **Make the reveal the share card** (pattern 1). What is on screen is what is exported: the 9:16 clip with the path, the number and the wordmark. "SHARE" goes straight to the existing story preview. No separate card-building step between the reveal and the share sheet.
6. **Fold the detail with the answers showing** (pattern 5). The detail screen starts with collapsed rows that already carry their value, as Warby Parker does: "Bar path · 4 cm forward at the knee", "Peak speed · 1.84 m/s", "Joints · tracked on 96% of frames". Each opens to its chart and controls.
7. **A named state for "body tracked, no bar path"** (pattern 7). This is a poor result, not a failure, and a quarter of finished tracks land here. Reveal copy: "We could not follow the plate." then one cause chosen from what the tracker knows, in Delta's manner: "The plate left the top of the frame." or "The plate was hidden behind another lifter." or "The plate was too small in the frame. Move closer." Buttons: "TRY ANOTHER CLIP" and "SEE THE JOINTS ANYWAY". State that it did not use the weekly video, if that is the rule.
8. **Put "Not right?" on a good result too** (pattern 7). A small text control under the actions, as Warby Parker's "Rescan" and Ulta's thumbs: "Path looks wrong?" It opens the same cause list and the existing "Send this clip" option.

## What to avoid

- **Confetti on a result that has not earned it.** Bevel rains confetti and a clapping emoji on a ten-minute session rated "Little physical demand" (https://mobbin.com/screens/ffce8bfe-c19b-4978-a37f-a858f29bcffa). Catchline's reveal is the path drawing itself. Decoration on top competes with it, and it rings false on a missed lift.
- **A grade on the lift.** Acorns shows "75% / Good!" with "The community average is 80%", and QUITTR shows a red bar against an "Average" bar. Catchline tracks; it does not judge technique in 4.0. A band word or a community average would be a claim the tracker cannot support, and it would sit badly beside the "AI estimate" label.
- **Explaining the result only after sign-up.** Breathwrk: "We'll show you what this means and how to improve it after sign up" (https://mobbin.com/screens/daa63b01-b6a9-4ff1-a681-693e9f88cbb9). The reveal is where the free lift proves the product. The meaning must be on it.
- **A table of equal-weight stats as the first view.** Apple Fitness (seven stats in a grid, https://mobbin.com/screens/08f4f651-0a4f-488f-91e6-c2ad9c341241) and Google Health (five rows of the same size) suit someone who checks in daily and knows the metrics. They are the equivalent of opening on the dense player.

## Searches run

Screens (iOS):
- "fitness app workout complete summary with one large hero number and share button" (deep, 16)
- "result screen comparing to your previous best, new personal record, faster than last time" (deep, 16)
- "scan results score reveal screen after AI analysis of photo or body scan" (deep, 16). About half were quiz scores, off topic.
- "analysis failed or could not detect, explaining why with tips and a try again button" (deep, 16). About half were generic network errors with a retry and no explanation; not used.
- "sleep or recovery score reveal with one sentence explanation and scroll down for details" (standard, 14)

Flows (iOS):
- "fitness app finishing a workout or run, summary screen then sharing it" (5 flows: Gentler Streak, Fitbit, Garmin Connect, Strava, Bevel). All start from an existing activity and go to share. Garmin's includes an aspect-ratio step ("Full Portrait (9:16)", "Vertical (4:5)", "Square (1:1)") before the image is chosen: https://mobbin.com/flows/29d268a9-9eb6-49d3-9878-b70605a4aebe

Not found: a swing, form or movement analysis result (golf, tennis, lifting). Study 27 already recorded that SwingVision, Onform and similar are not on Mobbin, and nothing new surfaced. No screen in this study reveals a result as a video with an overlay; the reveal recommendation combines the share-card pattern (Hevy, pushr, Runbuds) with the reveal beat (Cleo, Lemonade). No "partial result" screen was found either: recommendation 7 is built from failure and redo screens.
