# Mobbin study, 17 November 2026

Nine notes on how other iOS apps handle the moments Catchline is working on, written from Mobbin on the last day of access. Each note has the patterns seen, real examples with their Mobbin links and exact wording, specific changes for Catchline, what to avoid, and the searches that were run.

Read these with the first-lift baseline in `TASKS-referrals.md`: a third of installs never start a track, a quarter of finished lifts get no bar path, and the result opens on the full player.

The screenshots (165 files) are in the private research folder, not in this repository: they are other companies' screens, and Mobbin's image links expire after 30 days. Example numbers inside suggested copy (such as "1.82 m/s") are placeholders for layout, not measurements. Earlier Mobbin notes are studies 27 and 28 in the private research folder.

| Note | Patterns | The most useful thing |
|---|---|---|
| [01. First run to first value](01-first-run-to-first-value.md) | 7 | Photoroom puts "Use a sample image" directly under "Select a photo", opens a sheet of six samples, and shows "Background removed!" before any account or paywall. Catchline needs the same second button: a bundled lift that tracks in seconds, so nobody leaves without seeing a bar path. A third of installs never start a track. The cause the screens point to is that the first action needs footage the person may not have, and Catchline offers no other way in. |
| [02. Waiting and processing](02-waiting-and-processing.md) | 7 | the best waits show the person's own material being worked on and write what has been found so far. CapCut prints a running description of the footage beside the percentage; Noom shows the heart rate while the face scan is still running. Catchline can do this honestly, because the plate and the joints are found frame by frame: draw them on the lifter's clip as they are found, so the minute of waiting is the first half of the reveal. |
| [03. Result reveal](03-result-reveal.md) | 7 | Hevy's first-workout screen is the model. One line ("This is your 1st workout"), the result already laid out as a share card with three numbers, the share targets in a row under it, and a single "Done". The detailed screen comes after. Catchline opens on the dense player. It should open on the clip playing once with the path drawn, one number, and Share, Save and "See the detail", with the twelve controls one tap away. |
| [04. Share cards and share flows (Mobbin, 17 Nov 2026)](04-share-cards-and-social.md) | 8 | The most useful thing: the apps that get shared put the share card on the first screen after the result, already rendered, with one-tap named destinations under it (Hevy, Runbuds, Locket). Nobody asks the user to go and find a share button. Second: no app seen prints an invite code on the card itself. The brand mark and the user's handle go on the card; the invite travels as a link button beside it (Future Pro) or in the copied text (Hevy). Earlier notes (`27-mobbin-fitness.md`, section 1.12) already cover Runna's carousel, Future Pro's Guest Pass Link, Garmin's Share / Save / Copy, Hevy's first-workout card, NRC's badge and pushr. Those are cited again here only where a new detail was seen. |
| [05. Referral and invite screens (Mobbin, 17 Nov 2026)](05-referral-and-invite.md) | 8 | The most useful thing: the strongest invite screens say the whole deal in one two-part headline ("Give one month of Plus free. Get one month of Pro free."), show the code or link in full, and have one button. Everything else is a short "How it works" below the fold. Second: the best-placed ask is not in settings. Opal asks straight after showing the user a good result ("Help someone do the same."), and KOHO asks right after a successful action. |
| [06. Rating and feedback prompts (Mobbin, 17 Nov 2026)](06-rating-and-feedback-prompts.md) | 8 | The most useful thing: a two-button pre-prompt ("are you enjoying push?" / "not really" / "yes") shown after a good result, where "yes" leads to the system rating sheet and "not really" leads to a short feedback form. It costs one small sheet and keeps unhappy users out of the App Store. Second: Alta's one sentence, "We are a small team of 7 trying to build the best closet app, so a rating goes a really long way!", is the best "why" seen. Catchline can say the same thing more truthfully. |
| [07. Paywalls and limits](07-paywalls-and-limits.md) | 8 | The most useful thing: the limit screen should be a small sheet over the lifter's own clip that says how many free videos were used, the day the next one arrives, and three ways forward (Pro, invite, wait). Brainly, ChatGPT and Ahead all state the reset time in plain words, and none of the scarcity-timer paywalls would suit Catchline. Date: 17 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens only. Round 2 notes (file 27 in the private research folder, sections 1.14 and 1.15) already covered Hevy's three tiles, AllTrails, Strava and Centr trial rails, Duolingo plan labels, Recime, Fuse, Otter, Mimo and Vivid. Those are only repeated here where a new detail was read off the screen. |
| [08. Video player and analysis tools](08-video-player-and-analysis-tools.md) | 9 | The most useful thing: the apps that put many tools on a video keep the video bare and put the tools in a bottom sheet that starts collapsed (Future Pro, Slopes, TikTok's editor). Every tool in the sheet has a word under its icon, and the best ones show their current value in the label ("Speed (1x)"). Date: 17 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens only. Round 2 notes (file 27 in the private research folder, sections 1.4, 1.7 and 1.8) already covered MasterClass and Google Photos speed controls, Lovi's step chips, the Premier League scrub thumbnail, Fabric's drawing tools, HBO Max key moments, and the Photoroom, Wise, Hers and Alta compare screens. Mobbin still has no golf, tennis, running-form or lifting analysis app: a direct search for them returned workout class videos and run charts only. So this file is built from workout players, video editors, photo editors and activity charts. |
| [09. Library, progress and reasons to return](09-library-progress-and-return.md) | 9 | The most useful thing: the fitness apps that bring people back on a weekly rhythm count weeks, not days (komoot, Strava), and they ask for notifications by showing the exact notification and letting the user pick their training days (Any Distance). Catchline's free video already resets weekly, so the reset is the natural reminder. Date: 17 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens only. Round 2 notes (file 27 in the private research folder, sections 1.9, 1.10, 1.11, 1.17, 1.19 and 1.20) already covered The Outsiders and Gymshark charts, Noom's goal line, Bevel's strength delta, Hevy's feed card and exercise history, Duolingo and Gentler Streak streaks, Runna and Nike empty states, and Hevy, Future Pro and pushr widgets. This file adds screens not cited there. |
