# 05. Referral and invite screens (Mobbin, 17 Nov 2026)

The most useful thing: the strongest invite screens say the whole deal in one two-part headline ("Give one month of Plus free. Get one month of Pro free."), show the code or link in full, and have one button. Everything else is a short "How it works" below the fold.
Second: the best-placed ask is not in settings. Opal asks straight after showing the user a good result ("Help someone do the same."), and KOHO asks right after a successful action.

## Patterns

### 1. A two-sided headline that is the whole offer
The headline states what the friend gets and what the user gets, in that order or side by side. No reading needed below it.

- WHOOP: "1 month free for you, 1 month free for them". Sub-line "Refer friends to WHOOP and get a free month when they become a member." Buttons "SHARE REFERRAL LINK" and "INVITE CONTACTS". https://mobbin.com/screens/99bdda04-4814-48cc-b1c5-44ce179cc5f5
- Cherrypick: "Give one month of Plus free. Get one month of Pro free." One button "Share invite link". https://mobbin.com/screens/b5e5a271-808b-4b97-949c-7ec1eb940457
- Starlink: "Free month for you and them", with two large counters above it, "Users referred 0 users" and "Months of service awarded 0 months", and a QR button beside "Share". https://mobbin.com/screens/260e7ba9-4ecd-4d94-ae8c-4cac41aa18d5
- Jomo: "Gift 14 days. Get Jomo Plus for life." https://mobbin.com/screens/9a635a79-97d8-4d3e-a39f-552cfc901c61

Why it works: the reward is concrete and symmetric. "Give" comes first, which makes the message to the friend a gift and not a favour asked.

### 2. The invite is drawn as an object: a pass or gift card
The offer is shown as a card with the product name, the length and who it is from. It looks like something worth sending.

- Wispr Flow: "Gift Flow to a friend", a green card reading "Flow Pro", "UNLIMITED WORDS FOR 1 MONTH", "Gifted by Alex Smith". Three-line "How it works", the link with "Copy", button "Share Invite". https://mobbin.com/screens/daeb9e5e-a819-43e1-b4e1-8d050490a14e
- Tempo: a tilted card "30 Days free", "Guest Pass". Text "Give a guest pass to a friend. Get a month free when their paid subscription starts." https://mobbin.com/screens/cdf5e90f-c595-4d34-aa1f-808d36818ef8
- Ladder: a yellow "GUEST PASS" card, "REFER FRIENDS, GET LADDER APPAREL", "1st Share = Hat, 2nd Share = T-Shirt, 3rd Share = Sweatshirt", button "TEXT YOUR LINK". https://mobbin.com/flows/3c1da5b4-cf18-4223-9d88-24e9bf6d4cb3
- Opal: a card "Invite by Epidote5687", "Gift 30 Days of Opal PRO, for free!". https://mobbin.com/screens/29c7320a-f815-466c-8066-4339caf22384
- Noom: "GUEST PASS, 2 Weeks Free", button "Send Complimentary Pass". https://mobbin.com/flows/5f9bc0b3-ca8b-47dc-a415-6339e87aba4d

Why it works: "guest pass" frames the invite as access the member owns and hands out. The sender's name on the card makes it personal.

### 3. The code is large, visible and one tap to copy
The code or link is printed in full on the screen, with a copy control and a separate share button.

- Opal: "Your Referral Code" as five tiles "9 R P Y 6", then "Share Invite", then a row Copy Link, Messages, TikTok, Snapchat, Messenger. https://mobbin.com/screens/29c7320a-f815-466c-8066-4339caf22384
- Jomo: "FA2ZG4" in heavy type with a copy glyph, pinned above "Share your code"; a green "Copied!" banner at the top after the tap. https://mobbin.com/screens/9a635a79-97d8-4d3e-a39f-552cfc901c61
- Instacart: after sharing, "Code S44F8FC copied!" under the buttons "Send a text" and "Share", with "$0 earned." above. https://mobbin.com/screens/803d323a-b046-4681-be4a-d058bcfaf9af
- NordVPN: the link in a grey field with a copy icon, "Share referral link" under it. https://mobbin.com/screens/f18fd4b3-39aa-4a83-8951-9bbdc8e4e1c3

Why it works: a short code can be said out loud in a gym. Copy and share are different needs: one for pasting into a caption, one for sending now.

### 4. Progress and milestones on the same screen
A count of friends joined and a ladder of rewards, with locked steps visible.

- Jomo: "Referrals 0", then a numbered ladder: 1 friend, 3 friends, 5 friends, 10 friends each "Unlock this special icon", and 25 friends for lifetime. https://mobbin.com/screens/0f1fde0d-977a-4c00-adce-115f298ce363
- Numo: "Rewards": "Free month, 1 friend" (shown as "Redeemed"), "Free 6 months, 3 friends", "Free lifetime, 10 friends", then a "Friends (1)" list with names and dates. https://mobbin.com/screens/48400223-cd8b-479e-96a8-ccd01096ede4
- Origin: "Refer friends, get a lifetime membership." with a "Limited time offer" tag and a gold "FREE! FOR LIFE" ticket; three numbered steps ending "Unlock lifetime access"; counters "Annual subscribers 0", "Signed up 0". https://mobbin.com/screens/a30d055d-ff12-4dcd-be9e-f26a3f9a16e2 and https://mobbin.com/screens/a00cf8f5-505f-4189-9ad2-e47f0b793bda
- Rocket Money: "You have 3 months of free Premium to earn." with a bar "0/3 Months Free" and the note "Excludes pending referrals." https://mobbin.com/screens/a70b4ae3-0f10-4375-9f24-7238107ee442
- Opal: a ladder to "20 FRIENDS, 2 Years of Opal Pro" and "50 FRIENDS, Lifetime of Opal Pro". https://mobbin.com/screens/8c8e30d8-6c2e-46ca-bdc8-21e259e21350
- Future Pro: "Referral Credits" with three columns, Lifetime, Pending, Trial in Progress, and the empty state "No Guest Pass referrals yet". https://mobbin.com/flows/0cc28f14-f64d-48f2-befc-e5457c4d7887

Why it works: the first reward is one friend away, which is reachable. A lifetime tier at the top gives keen users a goal. Showing "pending" stops support questions.

### 5. Ask after a win, not only in settings
The invite appears at a moment when the user has just seen value.

- Opal: at the end of a results story, "You're improving 86.8% better than people similar to you." then "Help someone do the same.", the invite card, "0/5 GIFTS SENT", "Share Invite". https://mobbin.com/screens/584269f6-f69b-464f-a94f-99cd95d08f6a
- KOHO: straight after a "Money sent!" toast, a sheet with a "3-month Everything Pass, $66 Value" card, "Share invite link" and "Not right now". https://mobbin.com/screens/1a156242-96c7-4ba2-a63f-c51b9e888dd8
- Tempo and Mimo: an "Invite a friend" step inside onboarding with "Skip" ("Mimo 14-Day guest pass", "Invite a friend", "Skip for now"). https://mobbin.com/screens/cdf5e90f-c595-4d34-aa1f-808d36818ef8 and https://mobbin.com/screens/a03d162b-1cc0-4c58-b942-0de8bf492615
- Future Pro: "Guest Pass Link" on the workout share sheet (see file 04). https://mobbin.com/screens/bf2ae115-910c-4564-8758-e3860b24459c

Why it works: the user has a fresh reason to recommend the app, and the invite reads as "pass this on".

### 6. A standing entry point as a card in the profile
One card near the top of the profile or account tab, with the reward in the title.

- Alma: under the profile header, "Share Alma, get a month free", "You get 1 month of Premium free when a friend joins.", a gift icon and a share glyph. https://mobbin.com/screens/80e5c289-9a37-4813-8659-ff6c41dc3400
- WHOOP: profile section "Referrals": "Start Referring Friends", a tag "+1 month free per referral", button "REFER NOW". https://mobbin.com/screens/bce6a92b-c04f-45cc-8026-fa6c472c2e38
- Cal AI: a row under the name, "Refer a friend and earn $10". https://mobbin.com/screens/a038952d-b542-490f-87d1-a903cc801e14
- Runna: a gift icon in the top right corner of the profile. https://mobbin.com/screens/49c24d61-a870-4481-bbac-f56a0f90d537
- Headway: a card "Grow together. Share a 7-day free guest pass with your friends", button "Invite friends". https://mobbin.com/flows/d008d6f4-a3ec-411d-b016-d0696f30d919

Why it works: people look for it there when a friend asks "what app is that?". The reward in the title does the selling.

### 7. Entering a friend's code: optional, one field, skippable
A single optional field during onboarding, and the same field reachable later from the profile.

- Jomo: "Do you have a referral code?", one field, "Continue". https://mobbin.com/screens/92a92034-f129-4b0e-b59d-1495c40572a7
- Cal AI: "Enter referral code (optional)", "You can skip this step", the main button is "Skip". https://mobbin.com/screens/d22a8f5f-a3d9-4db9-841d-40618b367234
- Runna: a sheet over the profile, "Enter your code", "Enter a valid referral code to unlock free weeks of Runna premium", button "Verify". https://mobbin.com/screens/044105a3-3141-455f-8cc4-e8937d1718d2
- Kraken: a text link "Have a referral code?" opens a sheet: "A code is not necessary to continue, but if you have one, please enter it here." https://mobbin.com/screens/0bc541e0-776d-4b49-970c-762f375e32f0

Why it works: it never blocks. The copy says what the code unlocks, so a user with a code knows it is worth typing.

### 8. Confirm the reward in plain words, at once
After a valid code, the screen states what was added and until when.

- Forest: an inline green box "Valid promo code. Your promo code is valid and you can now enjoy 180 free minutes of bike riding!" and a toast "Minutes added". https://mobbin.com/screens/f833cc28-e53e-47c3-8fdf-fed9827acc9d
- Runna: "You've added a referral code" with the code, and the profile banner changes to "Alex, join Runna Premium. Runna has given you 2 weeks free. Claim yours now". https://mobbin.com/screens/49c24d61-a870-4481-bbac-f56a0f90d537
- Places: "Your first month of Places is on us." with a member card showing the name and "ISSUED ON" date, and "We'll check back in after 30 days." https://mobbin.com/screens/267427f9-27e2-415d-a7b3-9e66b84c5156
- Wealthsimple: "Referral code added! Once eligible, your reward will be available to claim in the app within 24 hours." https://mobbin.com/screens/78e733d5-c704-4384-b214-fecd3f8c3e81

Why it works: the friend's first minute confirms the sender told the truth. Stating the end date avoids a surprise later.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on sharing and on the first lift.

1. **One invite screen with the offer as the headline (pattern 1).** Title in display type: "GIVE A MONTH OF PRO. GET A MONTH OF PRO." Sub-line: "When a friend tracks their first lift with your code, you both get a month." One button "SHARE INVITE". Nothing else above the fold except the code.
2. **Show the code as tiles with copy (pattern 3).** Six characters in mono tiles, "TAP TO COPY" under it, then "COPIED" for two seconds. The share message: "Track your bar path with Catchline. Use my code XXXXXX for a month of Pro." plus the link.
3. **Ask on the first-lift result and on the share sheet (pattern 5).** After the first tracked lift: a single line under the share buttons, "Know someone who lifts? Give them a month of Pro." with "COPY INVITE LINK". Not a modal. Do not show it when the track failed or no bar path was found.
4. **A guest pass card (pattern 2).** On the invite screen and in the link preview: a black card, bone text, "CATCHLINE PRO", "1 MONTH", "FROM <NAME>" (or "FROM A LIFTER" for anonymous accounts), a thin red rule. This is also the image for the link's preview.
5. **Counters and a short ladder (pattern 4).** Under the code: "FRIENDS JOINED 0", "MONTHS EARNED 0", "PENDING 0". If milestones are wanted, three steps at most: 1 friend, a month; 3 friends, three months; 10 friends, a Founding member seat while seats remain. The last step is a product and money decision for Dani and Yash, not settled here.
6. **Entry card on the You tab (pattern 6).** A row near the top: "Give a month, get a month" with the code on the right. On the paywall, one text link under the plans: "Have an invite code?"
7. **Code entry that never blocks (pattern 7).** The paywall link and a You tab row "Enter invite code" open a sheet: title "INVITE CODE", one field, button "APPLY", line "A code gives you a month of Pro." No code step in onboarding; a third of installs already never start a lift, so nothing extra goes before the camera.
8. **Confirm with dates (pattern 8).** On a valid code: "A MONTH OF PRO, FROM <NAME>." and "Pro until 17 Dec 2026. No card needed." Button "TRACK A LIFT". To the sender, when the friend qualifies: a notification and a row "<Name> joined. Pro extended to <date>."

## What to avoid

- **Cash rewards and "earn up to" headlines** (Sweatcoin "Invite and earn up to $1,000", Klarna "Earn $60, give $20", Greenlight's $30 to $300 ladder). They read as a scheme and attract people who do not lift. Catchline's reward should stay the product.
- **Long terms above the button** (Rocket Money's three-step explainer plus T&Cs, GoPay's four paragraphs). The rules belong one tap away.
- **Rewards that need a human to fulfil** (Opal: "Contact us to get 2 years of Opal Pro"; Numo: instructions "will be emailed within 2 business days"). For a one-person team every manual reward is support work. Grant it automatically or do not offer it.
- **A mandatory or prominent code step in onboarding** (QUITTR, Fetch and Setel put a code screen in the sign-up path). It adds a step before the first lift and makes users without a code feel they are missing a deal.
