# 01. First run to first value

Mobbin study, 17 November 2026, iOS only. Adds to the October notes (study 27, sections 1.1 to 1.4, and study 28, section 11); screens cited there are only repeated where a new detail was read.

**The most useful thing:** Photoroom puts "Use a sample image" directly under "Select a photo", opens a sheet of six samples, and shows "Background removed!" before any account or paywall. Catchline needs the same second button: a bundled lift that tracks in seconds, so nobody leaves without seeing a bar path.
A third of installs never start a track. The cause the screens point to is that the first action needs footage the person may not have, and Catchline offers no other way in.

## Patterns

### 1. A sample sits next to the real action, as the second button

The first-use screen has two buttons of equal width: use your own, or use ours. The sample is not hidden in a menu or an empty state.

- Photoroom, onboarding step "Start from a photo". Headline "Start from a photo", sub-line "Magically remove the background from one of your pictures.", primary "Select a photo", secondary "Use a sample image". https://mobbin.com/screens/9afa0247-d8d0-48c6-a2e3-2d96e7900f3c
- Photoroom, the sheet that the second button opens: "Choose one of these images" over a grid of six samples (earrings, a car, a dress, a toy, a burger, a portrait). Then "Just like magic / Background removed!" with one button, "Start creating". Flow: https://mobbin.com/flows/80139c78-5c46-460c-96e2-8f0d9f576f57
- Once, home screen on first use. The "Active" section is empty ("No active films", "Create film"), but the "Albums" section below already holds an album called "Welcome!" with a small "SAMPLE" tag and four photos. https://mobbin.com/screens/d0ad4180-cee4-4ae3-aa13-f598df3ef6f2
- Halide Mark III, "Pick Your Look". A stock portrait is provided with the line "This is a normal iPhone photo. Tap the icons below to pick from Halide's exclusive looks." The person plays with the product on a supplied photo before the camera opens. https://mobbin.com/screens/2a69704d-ca91-4ba3-9cbd-4f5dbc6dc28b

Why it works: the result no longer depends on what is in the camera roll. Six samples rather than one lets the person pick the one closest to their own use, which makes the result feel like a preview of theirs.

### 2. The first screen after the splash names the one action, in the person's terms

No tour. One sentence that says what the first result will be and one or two buttons that start it.

- Alta, after sign-up: "Sam, your first outfit is one tap away", sub-line "Add a piece by photo, or search to add instantly.", buttons "Add with photos" and "Search items". A phone mock above shows what the result looks like. https://mobbin.com/screens/9a9acf15-91a0-4491-9851-f4f29c7267ea
- Shop (Get the Look mini): "Find every piece from any outfit", "Upload an outfit photo and discover matching pieces from Shopify stores", one button "Choose Photo". https://mobbin.com/screens/25008801-6557-4c18-87cb-37b19ce562b7
- Life Reset, food log: the chosen photo fills the screen, primary "Analyze Food", and "Camera" and "Library" as two equal secondary buttons under it. Source choice and the action live on one screen. https://mobbin.com/screens/1a5cd660-ebab-4c80-8c59-6bf6b3955e79
- Ulta Beauty GLAMlab: "Choose your mode." with three stacked buttons, "LIVE TRY ON MODE", "TAKE A PHOTO", "CHOOSE FROM CAMERA ROLL". https://mobbin.com/screens/506bbdab-2b3f-4083-8879-fa498be91fdb

Why it works: "one tap away" is a promise about effort. Camera and library as equal buttons stop the import path from feeling second class, which matters for lifters who already have clips.

### 3. Sign-up is skippable on the first screen, in plain words

The account screen carries a visible way past it.

- Brink: "Sign in to keep your shows, playback, and listening history in sync." Buttons "Sign in with Apple" and "Continue as Guest". The sentence says what the account is for, so skipping is an informed choice. https://mobbin.com/screens/9aed7539-9978-48bd-bd33-f8eed5398de8
- ChatGPT: three sign-in buttons, then a plain text "Skip" under them. Sub-line "Log in for the full experience." https://mobbin.com/screens/59a15eeb-abda-4e91-b22e-13d3de9b04fc
- Trainline: sign-in sheet with "Skip" in the top right of the sheet. https://mobbin.com/screens/ea1c8e35-9a4f-4bb3-b930-12a68ae1ab37
- Breathwrk does the reverse and gates the meaning of a result: "We'll show you what this means and how to improve it after sign up". Noted under "What to avoid". https://mobbin.com/screens/daa63b01-b6a9-4ff1-a681-693e9f88cbb9

Why it works: Catchline already starts anonymous, which is ahead of most of these. The lesson is the Brink sentence: when Sign in with Apple is offered later, say what it keeps.

### 4. Permission priming that states the narrowest true claim

The screens that read as safe say exactly what is touched and what is not, in one line, right beside the system sheet.

- Locket: the system sheet is shown inside the priming screen with the wanted button highlighted. Below it: "Locket needs photo library access so you can pick your photos." and a padlock line, "Only photos you pick are shared". https://mobbin.com/screens/c1676e91-e343-4374-adb1-beeb316832f3
- Halide Mark III: "TO GET STARTED, HALIDE NEEDS YOUR PERMISSION:" with three checkbox rows (camera, photo library, location marked "Optional."), each with a one-line reason. Footer: "Halide does not save, share, or upload any of your photos or information. Tap to learn more." https://mobbin.com/screens/ada9e43a-f026-49f2-a19a-40be5d9bc673
- Craft: a small card, "Need Permissions", one row "Photo Library / Required to upload photos from library", an "ALLOW" button in the row, and "We handle your data with care. We do not scan or store any information from your device." https://mobbin.com/screens/e7a3a849-0418-4622-8ffa-262b60847564
- Turo uses the system picker, which shows Apple's own banner: "Private Access to Photos. Your photo library is shown here, but 'Turo' can only access the items you select." No app permission prompt appears at all. https://mobbin.com/screens/f91e60ca-ad35-4885-9ce9-5b3b047ff6dd

Why it works: "only the ones you pick" and "does not upload" are claims a person can check against what they know. The Turo screen is the strongest case: with the system picker there is no photo permission to ask for, so the scariest prompt disappears.

### 5. Wrong and right shown as a pair of photos

Filming guidance is given as two pictures side by side, a cross over one and a tick over the other, one rule per screen.

- Tempo, "How to scan", 1 of 6: "Set your phone at waist height. Prop it up with the front facing you and in a vertical surface, then step away." Left photo, phone on the floor, red cross. Right photo, phone on a shelf at waist height, green tick. https://mobbin.com/screens/0a51ce57-b865-4f8f-9595-d55b1ba8ba9d
- Tempo, "Let's set up your phone", 3 of 3: "Positioning your phone. Place your phone in the portrait orientation." Hand-held landscape crossed, propped portrait ticked. https://mobbin.com/screens/e77b4ca9-5160-46df-a605-b35e43dead6e
- Alta, "Body Pic": one large ticked photo of a full-body mirror shot and two small crossed ones (a selfie, two people). "Snap a full body pic from head to toe with nobody else in the frame." Buttons "Upload" and "Skip for now". https://mobbin.com/screens/d156d2c6-22e4-4e76-92a7-c7a0b8960397
- Setel: a two-by-two grid, one ticked ("Bright & visible") and three crossed ("No eye cover", "No dark places", "No partial face"). https://mobbin.com/screens/6855dfd9-b415-481c-a117-2ee260c0125d

Why it works: a photo of the wrong set-up is recognised faster than a sentence about it. Tempo's waist-height pair is close to Catchline's real problem, since a quarter of finished tracks have no bar path and phone position is a likely cause.

### 6. Three short tips, then the action, with the reason for the tips stated

Where photos are not used, the guidance is three lines with icons and a button that starts capture.

- Cal AI: "Get the best scan:" then "Hold still", "Use lots of light", "Ensure all ingredients are visible", over a photo of someone doing it. Button "Next". https://mobbin.com/screens/7cc381b8-43ff-474b-9af2-16c9a9280127
- Starling, cheque photo: "Here's some tips on how to take a good photo of your cheque. If the photo isn't clear, we'll ask you to take another, which could delay your cheque being deposited." Four lines under "Please make sure:". Button "Take photo". https://mobbin.com/screens/232c3a9b-5040-4655-8a26-cc2cdb3cb153
- Walmart, glasses measurement: three rows with small pictures (well-lit space, sound on, camera access), primary "Measure now", text link "Enter manually". https://mobbin.com/screens/697eb667-dfd0-4cdb-824c-a54e0d9097ef
- UNIQLO body scan, 1 of 6: "Checklist to improve the accuracy of the scan." Six "no" icons (hats, outer layers, loose clothing, thick soles, dim light, objects hiding the body). https://mobbin.com/screens/049f2767-6c81-47c4-a1db-e60d0313645f

Why it works: Starling says what a bad capture costs the person ("we'll ask you to take another"). That gives the tips a reason. Walmart's "Enter manually" keeps a way forward for someone who cannot meet the conditions.

### 7. Tips stay reachable from inside the camera

The guidance is not only a gate before capture. A "Tips" control stays on the capture screen.

- Airbnb, ID capture: "Tips" in the top right of the viewfinder; state text in a chip under the frame ("Hold still"). https://mobbin.com/screens/db45321e-5bb6-48bd-b2ee-eda57904f3dd
- Fly Delta, passport: a "Scanning Tips" sheet sits at the bottom of the live camera with four lines (still, clean, whole page in frame, no glare). https://mobbin.com/screens/863dac0b-3b71-44c2-9eb7-0968c4d9f0c3
- Cash App and LinkedIn, ID capture: "Capture Tips" link under the frame, and the live problem written inside the frame ("Please move to a well-lit area.", "Blur detected, unable to capture"). https://mobbin.com/screens/3a43e2de-8bd1-448b-b547-d80ebea051c0 and https://mobbin.com/screens/ecb8c7e8-5bfc-4403-b1b4-589f67263886

Why it works: people skip the screen before the camera. The tips are needed at the moment the phone is being propped against a plate.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on the first lift.

1. **Add "Try a sample lift" as the second button on the first screen** (pattern 1). First screen: headline "SEE YOUR BAR PATH", sub-line "Film a lift or pick one from your library. It is tracked on your phone and stays there.", primary "FILM OR IMPORT A LIFT", secondary "TRY A SAMPLE LIFT". The second button opens a sheet, "Pick a lift", with four bundled clips (snatch, clean, squat, deadlift), as in Photoroom's six-image sheet. The sample opens straight on the reveal with a pre-computed track, so it takes seconds and does not use the free weekly video. Label it "SAMPLE" in the Lifts list, as Once does.
2. **End the sample on one button that starts their own lift** (pattern 1). Photoroom's result screen has a single "Start creating". Catchline's sample reveal ends with "NOW TRACK YOUR OWN" as the primary and "Share this" absent, since a sample is not theirs to share.
3. **Use the system photo picker and say so before it opens** (pattern 4). No photo-library permission prompt. One line above the import button: "You choose the clip. Catchline cannot see the rest of your library." For the camera, a Halide-style card with one row, "Camera: to film your lift", and the footer "Tracked on this phone. Nothing is uploaded."
4. **Replace the written filming advice with two Tempo-style pairs** (pattern 5). Screen 1 of 2: "Side on, hip height", crossed photo of a phone on the floor at an angle, ticked photo of a phone propped at hip height square to the bar. Screen 2 of 2: "Whole bar, whole body", crossed photo with the plate cut off at the top of a snatch, ticked photo with space above the lockout. Shown once before the first capture, with "Skip" in the corner.
5. **State what a bad angle costs, in Starling's manner** (pattern 6). Under the pairs: "If the plate leaves the frame, the bar path cannot be drawn." This is the line that addresses the quarter of finished tracks with no bar path.
6. **Keep "Tips" in the camera and on the trim screen** (pattern 7). A text control, top right, that reopens the two pairs. On import, the same control reads "Will this clip track?" and shows the two ticked photos.
7. **Camera and library as equal buttons on the capture entry** (pattern 2). As in Life Reset: "CAMERA" and "LIBRARY" at the same size and weight, since many lifters already hold clips from training.
8. **When Sign in with Apple is offered, say what it keeps** (pattern 3). Copy on the You tab: "Sign in to keep your lifts if you change phone." with "Not now" as plain text beside it.

## What to avoid

- **A quiz before the first action.** Photoroom asks "Do you sell physical products online?" before the sample step, and CapCut asks "What will you use CapCut for?". Both are short, but Catchline has one use and nothing to personalise. Any question screen before the first track adds to the third who never start.
- **Gating the meaning of a result behind sign-up.** Breathwrk shows the score, then "We'll show you what this means and how to improve it after sign up" (https://mobbin.com/screens/daa63b01-b6a9-4ff1-a681-693e9f88cbb9). For Catchline the first result is the whole pitch; hiding part of it trades the wow for an account.
- **A founder or coach welcome video.** Ladder, Centr, Runna and pliability open with a person talking to camera (for example https://mobbin.com/screens/621502c2-24e9-4cc9-bce9-bb348fa97d6b). It suits a coaching subscription. Catchline has no coach, and the video the lifter wants to see is their own.
- **A six-step preparation checklist.** UNIQLO's scan has six preparation screens. A lifter between sets will not read six. Two pairs is the ceiling.
