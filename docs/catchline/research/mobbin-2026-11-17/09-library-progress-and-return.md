# 09. Library, progress and reasons to return

The most useful thing: the fitness apps that bring people back on a weekly rhythm count weeks, not days (komoot, Strava), and they ask for notifications by showing the exact notification and letting the user pick their training days (Any Distance). Catchline's free video already resets weekly, so the reset is the natural reminder.

Date: 17 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens only. Round 2 notes (file 27 in the private research folder, sections 1.9, 1.10, 1.11, 1.17, 1.19 and 1.20) already covered The Outsiders and Gymshark charts, Noom's goal line, Bevel's strength delta, Hevy's feed card and exercise history, Duolingo and Gentler Streak streaks, Runna and Nike empty states, and Hevy, Future Pro and pushr widgets. This file adds screens not cited there.

## Patterns

### 1. A records page per movement, each record dated and tied to the session that set it

Each exercise has its own page. The page opens with the bests as plain rows, and a best links back to the workout it came from.

- Hevy, "Deadlift (Barbell)": tabs "Summary", "History", "How to", "Leaderboard". Under "Personal Records": "Heaviest Weight 100kg", "Best 1RM 106.74kg", "Best Set Volume 75kg x 8", "Best Session Volume 2000kg". Each value is a link. https://mobbin.com/screens/14db3357-172d-401e-9179-05f032ec2a26
- Strava, "Best Efforts": a row of distance chips, then "K1's 400m PR" with a gold medal, "45s", "1:52 /km", and directly under it the run that set it ("Lunch Run", with its map thumbnail and date). Second and third best follow with silver and bronze medals. https://mobbin.com/screens/59475482-3c4a-454c-bf44-936537dbd933
- Tonal, one exercise: tabs "Strength", "Power", "Volume", "Workouts". "1-REP MAX" on the left with "SINCE FEB 02, 2026", "59 lbs, STRENGTH PR (1RM)" on the right, a one-bar chart with a dated bubble, then the sets of the session below. https://mobbin.com/screens/49b9079a-87e6-434d-802e-25a9a9858077
- WHOOP, Strength Trainer: "Personal Records" as rows with a video thumbnail of the movement, its name, and "15 reps". https://mobbin.com/screens/c2fac635-d19d-492b-9393-101962e0fbf3

Why it works: a record with a date and a link is a memory, and tapping it replays the day. Strava's top three gives a second and third reason to open old sessions. The thumbnail (WHOOP) suits an app whose records are videos.

### 2. The full set of records shown from day one, with the empty ones visible

The records screen shows every slot. Earned ones have a value and a date. The rest are greyed and named.

- Runna, "Personal Records": a grid of hexagon badges. "1K, 2m 25s, Sep 6, 2025" and four more are filled in. "10K", "Half Marathon", "Marathon", "50K", "100K" are faded with no time. https://mobbin.com/screens/03da73d4-d1a8-4549-b9ba-ee68c011e05f
- Nike Run Club, "Achievements": "Personal Records" with "Farthest Run, Sep 25, 2025, 18.15" and "Longest Run, 2:41:00" in colour, then "Fastest 1K" and others as grey outlines. https://mobbin.com/screens/cd9f7c3b-983e-494f-8078-a6abe2efc90a
- Garmin Connect, "Personal Records": tabs per sport with a count badge on "Running". Rows "1 km, 7:00, 13/04/2026" with a blue dot for new, then "1 mi", "5K", "10K" as empty rows. https://mobbin.com/screens/215766d3-2e3f-446a-a209-8704edb2313f
- adidas Running, "RECORDS": sport filter chips, "OVERALL 4/4", four medallions each with a value and a label ("7.98 km, Farthest Distance"). "CYCLING 8/12" below. https://mobbin.com/screens/bf2b978e-c0ec-4df9-aa77-71affc711038

Why it works: after one session the screen is already partly filled, and every grey slot is a named thing to go and do. The "4/4" count turns a list into something that can be finished.

### 3. "Your best yet" said in a sentence straight after the session

The app states the record in words on the result, not only as a badge in a list.

- Nike Run Club: a full-screen card in one flat colour. A "3X" badge, the date, "YOU'RE ON FIRE", "You ran 3 times in a week.", and one button, "Share". https://mobbin.com/screens/931d5b5c-bc58-44f6-80e1-478c4ae9d594
- Ahead: after an exercise, "Great job! Can you do even better next time?" and under it "Personal record: 40". Button "All done". https://mobbin.com/screens/8246b3c6-7703-4f02-99fc-b16c8349f2cd
- Strava, Athlete Intelligence: a sheet over the run whose headline says the run had three new personal bests and met the weekly goal, followed by two paragraphs of detail. "Got it" and "Give Feedback". https://mobbin.com/screens/79781054-6bd6-4e50-bd30-726e9706c0a1
- Tonal, history: the week's bar chart has a bubble on the tallest bar, "BEST DAY, 40 mins". https://mobbin.com/screens/8815ce5b-8321-434d-afd7-3a8f55f81542

Why it works: the user finds out at the moment they care most. Nike's card has one fact and one button, which is the share. Ahead's line points at the next attempt. Strava's version shows the limit: two paragraphs is more than anyone reads after a session.

### 4. A weekly rhythm: weeks in a row and sessions per week

The habit unit is the week. The app counts weeks with at least one session, or sessions against a weekly target.

- komoot: "1 week streak", "Keep up the adventures each week to continue your streak." A "Tracker" card for the month with one dot per week, the current one ticked, the next labelled "Next week", and "Add to your streak next week with an activity of 15 min or more." A "Streak communication" switch below. https://mobbin.com/screens/edf52b1d-5764-46b7-9394-6521e997c49e
- Strava widgets: one widget shows "1 Weeks" beside "Your streak" and the days of this week. Another shows a ring with "3" and "Weekly goal: 2" with a bar per day. https://mobbin.com/screens/1c996718-ab94-4247-85b3-80885bab44c9
- Equinox+, "Session Adherence": a table with one row per week, "Required 3 / 3" and "Optional 1 / 4". https://mobbin.com/screens/ca471bd6-302c-42cd-98e3-05abf666a543
- Babbel, "Weekly summary": a count under each weekday letter, then "Weekly goal, You reached your goal" and "Continue learning". https://mobbin.com/screens/65435f22-30a8-440c-9868-8fb64d41c7e9

Why it works: a lifter snatches two or three times a week. A weekly count cannot be broken by a rest day, and komoot states the minimum plainly so the rule is never in doubt. It also matches a free plan with one video a week: one tracked lift keeps the run going.

### 5. This week against last week, as numbers first

The history tab opens on a short summary for the current period with a comparison, and the list sits below.

- Gentler Streak, "Activities": tabs "Week", "Month", "Year", "All Time". "This Week" with "vs. LAST WEEK" under it, a "SHARE" button, a chart that draws both weeks, then four tiles each with an up or level arrow ("Duration 1h 15m", "Active Energy 214 kcal"). "History, 1 ACTIVITY" follows. https://mobbin.com/screens/81ddb60b-a773-448c-b64a-92cef5221268
- Nike Run Club, "Activity": "W, M, Y, All" tabs, "Last Week", one very large figure "39.5 Miles", three small ones ("9 Runs", "10'03'' Avg. Pace", "6:37:18 Time"), a bar per day, then "Recent Activity". https://mobbin.com/screens/990cfe5c-7b29-4608-a26f-6797264cf2fd
- Strava, "You": "This week" with three figures, "Past 12 weeks" as a line with one dot per week, and "See more of your progress". https://mobbin.com/screens/8750eb55-deb4-4533-8b91-0a85affae067
- MyFitnessPal, "Weekly Digest": a sentence first ("You burned an average of 298 calories during each cardio exercise."), then plain rows of counts. https://mobbin.com/screens/8dce255f-648b-4a32-92f4-9cba7467236a

Why it works: one big number and a direction answer "am I doing more or less" without reading a chart. Strava's twelve dots show a season on one line, which works even when most weeks have one session.

### 6. The notification ask shows the notification and lets the user set the days

Before the system prompt, the app shows a mock of the exact notification and asks when the user trains.

- Any Distance: a mock banner "Today's Challenge, Time for a quick workout!", the line "Stay motivated with a daily reminder to exercise. Customize in settings any time.", then a switch and a time for each weekday ("Monday 8:00 AM" down to "Sunday", off). Button "Next". https://mobbin.com/screens/6aa76fdd-ccad-4ded-931a-2b896f3314f7
- Runna: a phone drawing with a banner "You have a workout scheduled today, Easy Run 4km: 30m-40m", and the claim "You're 2x more likely to finish your plan with workout reminders". Button "Continue". https://mobbin.com/screens/0e27688c-a120-48c0-8152-863aa0b6473a
- Gentler Streak: "Keep your momentum." with a mock banner "New Activity Summary, Details of your last run are now available for your inspection!". Buttons "Skip" and "Go for it". https://mobbin.com/screens/40740abe-04b9-4d43-a601-e9c521f3d6f5
- Ahead: a mock banner in the user's own context, "You have a plan! Let's make sure you remember it tomorrow", a time wheel, "Let's make it stick", "Not now". Small print: "Easily turn off reminders once 'Ahead time' has become second nature." https://mobbin.com/screens/82507060-99c2-4662-9495-011e1048ad43

Why it works: the user agrees to a specific message on specific days, which is a smaller thing than "notifications". Gentler Streak's example is about a result being ready, not a nag. Any Distance's per-day switches fit people who train on fixed days.

### 7. A widget offered with a preview and a plain way to decline

The app shows the widget with the user's own data in it and offers "Add" and "Not now". Steps come after, only if wanted.

- BitePal: "Bring Alex to your home screen", a drawn home screen with the widget showing "Calories left 1,056", "Add widget" and "Maybe later". https://mobbin.com/screens/69b5a69d-2172-48b2-a0a9-e0248ed03de4
- Insight Timer: "Search for Insight Timer and select a Widget", with the widget drawn inside a phone outline reading "Keep it going, 2 consecutive days" and the week's ticks. "Got it". https://mobbin.com/screens/e3255c92-e788-4dd5-bb81-ca664203b688
- Cal AI: a sheet with a "Home Screen / Lock Screen" switch, a preview ("Lock Screen Widget, Track calories from your lock screen"), then "How to add widget" as numbered pictures. "Done". https://mobbin.com/screens/24f7bfa5-c0d5-4287-af72-85277ff02685
- Hevy's widgets, for content: "4 Workouts, Last 7 days" with a circle per day, and "Volume 18.7k kg, Last 7 days" with small bars. https://mobbin.com/screens/79a622bd-392f-4ab4-9eb7-18e3a85fcc5c

Why it works: iOS cannot add a widget for the user, so the preview has to make the effort look worth it. Showing real data in the preview does that. None of these apps ask on the first screen: the widget needs something to show.

### 8. A library with one item keeps the next action pinned

With a single entry, the list shows that entry in full and a fixed button for the next one.

- Tempo, body scan "History": one row, "August 13, 2025 at 6:53 PM, 121 lbs, 27.8% fat, 72.2% lean", and a large fixed button "Start a new scan". https://mobbin.com/screens/d608a723-da03-4be5-b5c4-1990c794e9f4
- Taco Bell, "My Orders": one card with the date, the items, thumbnails, "ORDER AGAIN" and "VIEW DETAILS", and a fixed "START ORDER" bar. https://mobbin.com/screens/c81f3503-d4a9-41fb-a183-d67ee706001e
- MyFitnessPal, exercise list: one row and a fixed "Log more" button. https://mobbin.com/screens/f68d8d61-d035-43ff-8ec6-a135b7be7e3e

Why it works: the row carries its result (Tempo prints the three numbers), so the list is worth a look even with one entry, and the button means the empty space below is never a dead end. Lime's one-row history with nothing else on the screen (https://mobbin.com/screens/ec42f854-fbec-458e-b309-23fb6c2bff2d) shows the same state without the button.

### 9. A locked section says exactly how many more are needed

Where a feature needs more data, the screen names the count and gives the action.

- Tripadvisor, "Your milestones": a card "Reviews, Locked" with a scale "0 1 2 3 5 10 20 50 100+", the line "Write 1 review to unlock" and a "Write a review" button. https://mobbin.com/screens/7fc51dec-bba9-4a12-b457-2bff53febed5
- Ahead: a greyed radar chart, "Unlock your complete psychological profile and personalized insights.", and a ring "2/7" beside "Achieve a 7-day streak to unlock". https://mobbin.com/screens/674a49ac-d2d6-492c-998a-05cef5abf15a
- pillowtalk: two blurred cards, "log 2 more entries to reveal your next patterns", three dots labelled "entry 1", "entry 2", "entry 3" with the first filled, and "I'm locked in for 2 more!". https://mobbin.com/screens/234f2cb5-3b9a-41d3-bbf4-27bb3c9116f9
- Withings: "We do not yet have enough data to be able to provide your Health Improvement Score. Start tracking to gain access." with tiles "Activity 2 of 3", "Body 3 of 5". https://mobbin.com/screens/dbd5c8e5-224d-476b-a4b4-7e36c7262e77

Why it works: "not enough data" becomes a short, countable job. Gymshark's "Squat (PB) 0 lb, No Data Available" chart (https://mobbin.com/screens/fc1a8eac-366c-4b9a-97be-25d9e7fe69ed) is the same state with no count and no action.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on the first lift, then on return and sharing.

1. **Make the first lift a record by definition.** (Patterns 2 and 3.) The first tracked snatch is the lifter's fastest snatch. Say so on the first result, as the last beat of the reveal: a line in condensed display type, "FIRST SNATCH TRACKED", then "Peak bar speed 1.82 m/s. This is the number to beat." and the label "AI estimate". One button under it: "SHARE". From the second lift on, when the number is higher: "FASTEST SNATCH YET · 1.88 m/s · was 1.82 on 17 Nov".

2. **Give the one-lift Lifts tab a second row that is not a lift.** (Patterns 8 and 9.) Under the first lift's card (thumbnail with the bar path, movement, date, peak speed), show a dashed row: "LIFT 2" with "Next free video: Mon 23 Nov" for a free lifter, or "Film your next lift" for Pro. Under that, one line: "Two snatches unlock side by side." The Track button stays fixed at the bottom. With zero lifts the same dashed row reads "LIFT 1 · takes about a minute".

3. **Ask for notifications after the first result, tied to the weekly video.** (Pattern 6.) One screen, shown once, after the lifter leaves the first result. A mock banner at the top: "Catchline · Your free video is back. Film today's heaviest snatch." Headline: "WHEN DO YOU LIFT?" Seven day toggles (M T W T F S S), none pre-set, and one time. Line: "One reminder on the first day you lift each week. Nothing else." Buttons: "REMIND ME" and "Not now". The system prompt appears only after "REMIND ME". For Pro the banner reads "Snatch day. Film one and see if it beats 1.82 m/s."

4. **Add a Records block to the You tab with every movement shown.** (Patterns 1 and 2.) Four rows from day one: Snatch, Clean, Squat, Deadlift. A tracked movement shows its thumbnail, "Fastest bar 1.82 m/s", the load if entered, and the date, and opens that lift. An untracked one is greyed: "Clean · not tracked yet". A header count: "RECORDS 1/4". If load was entered, keep bests per load band, since bar speed at 60 kg and at 100 kg are not comparable.

5. **Put the record on the share video.** (Pattern 3.) When a lift sets a record, the 9:16 export gets a two-second end frame: "FASTEST SNATCH YET · 1.88 m/s" in the display type with the Catchline mark and the invite code. Nike's card proves the format: one fact, one share button.

6. **Count weeks in a row, not days.** (Pattern 4.) A week counts when one lift is tracked. On the Lifts header: "3 WEEKS IN A ROW". Tapping it opens a komoot-style card: one dot per week for the last eight weeks, this week ticked or open, and "Track one lift by Sunday to make it 4." A free lifter can keep this going with the weekly free video.

7. **Open the Lifts tab on a one-line week summary.** (Pattern 5.) Above the list: "THIS WEEK · 2 lifts · fastest snatch 1.88 m/s" and, when last week has a lift of the same movement and load band, "up 0.06 on last week". With no basis for comparison, show no arrow. Later, a twelve-dot line per movement as Strava does.

8. **Offer the widget after the third lift.** (Pattern 7.) A sheet with a drawn home screen and a small widget filled with the lifter's own data: "SNATCH · 1.88 m/s best" and "Free video: ready" or "back Mon". Buttons "ADD WIDGET" (opens three numbered steps) and "Not now". Asked once.

## What to avoid

- **Daily streaks with flames.** Sweatcoin ("1 day streak! Make it a 2-day streak to form a healthy habit", https://mobbin.com/screens/d50b4868-0442-4588-932d-1141e4218133), pushr ("keep working out every day to build up your streak", https://mobbin.com/screens/5cfa8827-6feb-446e-9758-c156382450e9) and BitePal count days. Nobody should snatch heavy every day, and a free lifter gets one video a week, so a daily count would be broken by design.
- **Percentile and level claims.** Hevy's "You are stronger than 48% of male lifters your age and bodyweight" needs a population of other users' data. Catchline tracks on the phone and has no such data. Any ranking would be invented.
- **Unproven "2x more likely" lines in the notification ask.** Runna ("You're 2x more likely to finish your plan") and Gymshark ("Users who set reminders are 2x more likely to complete Gymshark66", https://mobbin.com/screens/dcd80a03-200b-41b0-9025-648af63800d7) quote their own figures. Catchline has none yet. Describe the reminder and leave the statistics out.
- **Paragraphs of praise and confetti.** Strava's Athlete Intelligence sheet runs to two paragraphs of enthusiasm, and Withings and Numo throw confetti over a mascot or badge. Catchline 4.0 shows tracking only, with results labelled as estimates. One line and the number is the right size, in black, bone and red.

## Searches run

All `search_screens`, platform iOS.

1. "new personal record celebration screen in a workout or running app" (deep, 12). Half the results were daily-streak screens.
2. "weekly training summary or weekly recap screen in a fitness app" (deep, 12)
3. "fitness app screen asking to turn on workout reminders by choosing training days and time, before the notification permission" (deep, 12)
4. "onboarding screen showing how to add the app's home screen or lock screen widget" (standard, 12)
5. "personal bests or records list per exercise in a strength training app" (deep, 12)
6. "history screen with only one entry so far and a prompt to record the next one" (deep, 12). Mostly order and payment histories. Tempo was the one fitness result.
7. "weekly goal of sessions per week with a weeks-in-a-row streak, not a daily streak" (deep, 10)
8. "progress chart that needs more data, showing how many more sessions until the trend unlocks" (deep, 10). No fitness chart with an unlock count was returned. The examples are from other categories.

Not found: a "your fastest yet" moment for bar speed or any velocity measure, a library of the user's own analysed videos, or a widget shown during first-run onboarding in a fitness app. The widget screens seen were reached from settings or after some use.
