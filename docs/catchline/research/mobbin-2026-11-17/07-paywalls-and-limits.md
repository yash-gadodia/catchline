# 07. Paywalls and limits

The most useful thing: the limit screen should be a small sheet over the lifter's own clip that says how many free videos were used, the day the next one arrives, and three ways forward (Pro, invite, wait). Brainly, ChatGPT and Ahead all state the reset time in plain words, and none of the scarcity-timer paywalls would suit Catchline.

Date: 17 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens only. Round 2 notes (file 27 in the private research folder, sections 1.14 and 1.15) already covered Hevy's three tiles, AllTrails, Strava and Centr trial rails, Duolingo plan labels, Recime, Fuse, Otter, Mimo and Vivid. Those are only repeated here where a new detail was read off the screen.

## Patterns

### 1. Three plans in one row or stack, with lifetime as an equal third

Monthly, yearly and a pay-once plan sit side by side at the same size. The yearly plan is pre-selected and carries the only badge. Lifetime has a two-word explanation.

- Hevy: "Get full access to Hevy", six tick rows, three tiles "MONTHLY $2.99 Billed monthly", "YEARLY $23.99 Billed annually" under a "SAVE 33%" tab, "LIFETIME $74.99 Pay once". Button "Subscribe to Yearly plan", then "Not now", then "Cancel your subscription at any time." A "Skip" link sits top right as well. https://mobbin.com/screens/19fce77b-a3e7-428c-857c-79aa00fbe716
- Joi: headline is the feature list itself, "Unlock daily summaries, reminders sync, custom icons and more." Three stacked rows: "Monthly S$ 6.98", "Annual S$ 39.98" with a "-52%" tag, "Lifetime, Pay once, use forever, S$ 89.98". Button "Subscribe". "Restore purchases" and "Recurring billing · Cancel any time" below. "Skip" top right. https://mobbin.com/screens/f534ccc2-5c15-4ef1-b50c-b823dd786fa6
- Bloom: three dark tiles. "MONTHLY $9.99, $2.49/week, Billed monthly, No trial", "YEARLY $59.99, $1.15/week, Billed yearly, 7-day free trial" under "Most Popular", "LIFETIME $99.99, Billed once, Bloom for life, No trial". Footer "Change plans or cancel anytime". https://mobbin.com/screens/db6e7f44-adcb-48a5-a585-48df56e93efe
- 5 Minute Journal: one white card with three radio rows, "Annual, $39.99" tagged "BEST VALUE" with "$3.33 per month, billed annually, 3 days free trial", "Monthly, $9.99, Billed monthly", "Lifetime, $199.00, One time payment, yours forever". (The screen separates name and price with a dash.) https://mobbin.com/screens/7fff754e-0548-4edf-bcb4-e6693302e99b

Why it works: the lifetime price makes the yearly price look small, and a buyer who dislikes subscriptions has somewhere to go without leaving the screen. In every example lifetime costs between 1.7 and 5 times the yearly price (Bloom 1.7, Joi 2.3, Hevy 3.1, 5 Minute Journal 5). Catchline's founding seat is 1.5 times its yearly price, the cheapest ratio seen, so on Catchline the lifetime row is the bargain and not the anchor.

### 2. A limit sheet that counts, then says when the allowance returns

When the free allowance runs out the app shows a half sheet over the content the user was trying to open. The sheet has a count, a bar, the reset time, one buy button and a plain way out.

- Brainly: sheet over the question the user tapped. "You've used 10 of 10 free questions", "Unlock unlimited access for every answer.", a full green bar labelled "10 total" and "0 left this week", three tick rows, "SUBSCRIBE", "S$ 14.48/month. Cancel anytime.", "Maybe later". https://mobbin.com/screens/ba6ce0da-0545-485c-8138-fbe1f51bd414
- ChatGPT: "Usage and limits", a bar with "Usage limit, 0% remaining" and under it "Resets Wed 4:35 PM". https://mobbin.com/screens/3201d836-d76d-492b-b361-c84dc936076c
- Ahead: "Daily included usage, 94% remaining", "Refreshes in 2 hours", then a second card "Credits never expire and only get used after your daily included usage is done." and "Purchase more credits". https://mobbin.com/screens/dbee17fb-d099-432f-b798-8b920cc8a92c
- Lex: two cards. First "That's it for today. You've reached your limit for today, come back to discover more profiles tomorrow." Second, separate, "Or, put yourself in the spotlight with Boost!" with its own button. https://mobbin.com/screens/041aec93-c628-44da-8f52-80456d921236

Why it works: the user learns the rule at the moment it bites, sees that waiting is a real option, and the price sits next to the button. A named day and time ("Resets Wed 4:35 PM") reads as a promise. "Come back later" does not.

### 3. The result first, the gate after

The free user gets the real output, and the gate sits on what comes next or on extra detail inside the result.

- Breathwrk: after a breath test the screen shows "4.8 seconds", "Your Lung Health Score is 54" in a ring, then "We'll show you what this means and how to improve it after sign up" and "Save and Continue". The number is given before anything is asked. https://mobbin.com/screens/daa63b01-b6a9-4ff1-a681-693e9f88cbb9
- Coffee Meets Bagel: an "Activity Report" card inside a profile. Four metrics are named ("Chats with matches", "Sends first message", "Active within 72 hours", "Replies within"), each showing "Locked", with one "Upgrade now" button. https://mobbin.com/screens/cb679aa2-1632-4830-bd55-dc3ec03b4433
- Joi: in the day view the daily summary area is blurred with "Joi Pro, Subscribe to unlock, Subscribe to Joi Pro to unlock daily summaries" and a small "Upgrade" button. The rest of the day works. https://mobbin.com/screens/248ab029-77cd-4fcd-a6fd-cf9550331644
- Linktree: an "Unlock powerful insights" card with three ticks and "Try it for Free", then the real layout below filled with rows marked "*Sample data" and a "Pro" tag. https://mobbin.com/screens/305e4ac0-19b0-43a4-bde5-e459070c84e5

Why it works: the user has already seen the product work on their own data, so the upgrade is a request for more of something known. Naming each locked item tells them exactly what the money buys.

### 4. An invite route offered as a way past the wall

The app gives a non-paying way to get the paid thing, by bringing someone in, and states the terms for both sides in one line.

- Life Reset: sheet "Share a guest pass", "Friends who tap your link get 30 days free" followed by "paywall skipped." A large "TAP TO COPY LINK" block, "GUEST PASSES, 3 of 3 left" with three dots, a row "Post to Instagram Story, Tell your friends you've got passes to give.", then Messages, WhatsApp, More. Footnote: "A pass burns only when your friend redeems", then "not when you share." https://mobbin.com/screens/3dc9e319-4531-4e7a-864a-f8290f7f728d
- Numo: "Invite friends, use Numo for free!", "Send invites", a dashed box "When friends join, they get free 30 days, you get free lifetime", then a rewards ladder "Free month, 1 friend", "Free 6 months, 3 friends". https://mobbin.com/screens/e4cf86fb-112d-4af5-9c88-8a0342467ba3
- Shelf: inside a weekly recap, "Unlock your weekly recap forever", "Invite at least 1 friend to get your Weekly Recap every Monday", "Invite friends". https://mobbin.com/screens/4bfbe4b1-137a-4e85-aadc-d5f1acf4cd14
- Origin: "Refer friends, get a lifetime membership." with a numbered three-step rail and two buttons, "INVITE FRIENDS" and "COPY UNIQUE LINK". https://mobbin.com/screens/546013bb-8f72-4a9e-a128-d750b1a3423b

Why it works: a user who will not pay today can still do something useful for the app. Life Reset's "3 of 3 left" makes the pass feel like a thing to give away, and its footnote removes the fear of wasting one.

### 5. A one-off or early offer explained in plain sentences

Apps that sell a single purchase or an early-member deal say what it is, what it includes later, and why it exists. The ones that read as honest use sentences. The ones that read as pressure use timers.

- Up, "Up High" early access: "This one-off cost unlocks these benefits and any future additions to Early Access." and "When the full Up High subscription launches in the first half of 2025, Early Access members will get 3 months free. After that you'll have the choice to continue with a subscription or not." Button "Join Up High", link "Learn more and T&Cs". The benefit above it: "Your app will get a special Up High look and feel." https://mobbin.com/screens/3b512f86-368f-4fd2-b693-29737363d5b1
- Orbit: a single row "Lifetime" with a small "Early bird" tag and the price, "Continue", then "Pay once and enjoy forever". https://mobbin.com/screens/40d3a02c-2503-4cc1-b940-518ebc20f189
- Crouton: "Crouton Plus is a one time purchase across all of your devices on iOS & macOS. Purchase within the first 24 hours of installation and save." Button "Purchase Crouton Plus", then "Restore Purchase". https://mobbin.com/screens/cc8b13fb-a973-4b75-91f9-49f5e294fd67
- Locket: feature rows where one carries an "Early access!" tag, then "Plus so much more..." with "Early product updates", "Monthly feature drops", "Membership badge". Button "Upgrade your Locket", then "No thanks!". https://mobbin.com/screens/42ccb9e6-d515-44d3-87fe-cd26bc1ecbfe

Why it works: an early buyer is taking a risk on a young product. Up answers the three questions that buyer has (what do I get now, what do I get later, what happens when pricing changes). A badge or a special look gives the buyer something to show.

### 6. The maker speaks, and promises what stays free

Small apps put a signed note on the paywall that says what the free tier will always include and how the money is used.

- timespent: lower-case note, "not ready for a paid tier? that's ok. i'll still keep building for you, and i promise:" then "meaningful free tier", "no dark patterns" ("no intrusive pop-ups, no long ass sign-up flow that forces you to subscribe, betting you'll forget to cancel") and "clean, minimal design", a signature, and three round stamps "MONTHLY $5.98", "YEARLY $19.98", "FOREVER $59.98". "restore purchase" below. https://mobbin.com/screens/b00fa672-0e18-4d18-8040-8912c0af8ca7
- Jomo: three questions as headings, "How is Jomo different?", "Can I trust you with my data?" ("Your screen time data is handled by Apple and stays on your device."), "What if I can't stick to restrictions?". Button "Try FREE for 3 days, then S$ 44.98/year" and under it "We'll notify you 24h before your trial ends!". "Later" top right. https://mobbin.com/screens/355c903b-4580-4f82-bd8f-0c46195fa434
- (Not Boring) Timer: "More play. Less routine." with five rows ending "No ads" and "Support Indie", a single "Lifetime, $14.99 one-time purchase" row, "Continue", "All plans". "RESTORE" top left. https://mobbin.com/screens/1b898d31-140d-4d6c-a7fa-52a56f9fb6bf
- Halide (cited in round 2, same screen): "Every purchase supports Ben, the developer of Halide, and his family." and "Why is Halide not free?". https://mobbin.com/screens/699b087c-8c67-4fe6-8b91-360a080edace

Why it works: a $2.99 app from one maker cannot win on awards and review counts. It can win on being believed. Jomo's data answer is the same claim Catchline can make truthfully: the video stays on the phone.

### 7. Restore, skip and "not now" in fixed, quiet places

Restore sits in a top corner or directly under the plans as plain text. The way out is a text link under the buy button, in the buyer's words.

- Hevy: "Skip" top right and "Not now" under the button, both present. https://mobbin.com/screens/19fce77b-a3e7-428c-857c-79aa00fbe716
- 5 Minute Journal: "Restore" top left, "Skip" top right, one offer in the middle ("Try 3 Days, then $3.33 per month (billed annually as $39.99)", "No commitment, cancel anytime."), "Accept Free Trial". https://mobbin.com/screens/c5de87f4-a628-4e77-a937-6ddda93aadb4
- Brainly: "Maybe later" as the last line of the limit sheet. https://mobbin.com/screens/ba6ce0da-0545-485c-8138-fbe1f51bd414
- Flo: "Restore" top right, and in the footer a text link "choose limited access without full Flo benefits". https://mobbin.com/screens/517f6841-6396-424f-b9f0-d285e5d3736a

Why it works: nobody hunts for the exit, so nobody feels trapped. Flo's wording is the weak version, since it makes the free choice sound like a loss. "Not now" and "Maybe later" are neutral.

### 8. A lapsed or blocked state that says the user's work is safe

When access ends, the screen says first that nothing was lost.

- Savee: "Your plan expired", "Everything you saved is right where you left it.", "No ads, no distractions. Just inspiration.", button "Get back in", "From $9/month · Cancel anytime". https://mobbin.com/screens/6ebca455-a73e-4271-8aad-8df17ab7223e
- Brainly: the banner behind the limit sheet reads "Get premium access back!" and the question stays visible under the sheet. https://mobbin.com/screens/ba6ce0da-0545-485c-8138-fbe1f51bd414
- Forest: "Come back tomorrow! We're out of videos. Need more minutes? Grab a bundle to stock up instantly!" with "GET MORE MINUTES". https://mobbin.com/screens/b1fbdb71-07d6-4f6b-96e5-7303638ae4de

Why it works: a lifter who has just filmed a heavy single cares about that clip more than about the plan. Telling them it is kept turns a block into a delay.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on the first lift, then on sharing.

1. **Never show a price before the first bar path.** (Pattern 3.) No paywall in onboarding and none on the way to the first track. The first time plans appear is after the first result, as one line under the result: "This was your free video for the week. Next one: Monday." with a text link "See Pro". Breathwrk gives the score before it asks for anything, and a third of Catchline installs already drop before tracking.

2. **Only spend the weekly video when a bar path is drawn.** (Pattern 4, the Life Reset footnote.) A quarter of finished tracks have no bar path today. Those should not use up the allowance. Say so on the failure screen: "No bar path, so this one did not count. Your free video is still here." This is a rule change as well as copy, and it protects the first lift more than any paywall design.

3. **Build the limit as a half sheet over the trimmed clip.** (Patterns 2 and 8.) Shown when a free lifter taps Track with the week's video used. The clip stays visible behind it. Content, top to bottom:
   - Headline, condensed display: "THIS WEEK'S FREE VIDEO IS USED"
   - A one-segment bar, filled, labelled "1 of 1" on the left and "Next free video: Mon 23 Nov" on the right. Use the real date.
   - One line: "Your clip is saved. Track it on Monday, or now with Pro."
   - Primary button, 56pt, 4px radius: "TRACK NOW WITH PRO"
   - Secondary button, outlined: "INVITE A LIFTER. YOU BOTH GET A MONTH"
   - Text link: "Not now"
   The saved clip then shows in Lifts as a row "Waiting. Free on Monday" with a Track button that works on the day.

4. **One plan block, three rows, used everywhere plans appear.** (Pattern 1.) Stacked rows, not tiles, so the founding line has room for its sentence:
   - "Monthly · $2.99"
   - "Yearly · $19.99" with "$1.67 a month" under it and a small red "SAVE 44%" tag. Pre-selected.
   - "Founding member · $29.99" with "Pay once. Pro for good. 137 of 500 seats left." Use the live count.
   Button text follows the selection: "START YEARLY", "START MONTHLY", "TAKE A FOUNDING SEAT". Under the button: "Cancel any time in Settings" for the two subscriptions, "One payment. No renewal." for founding.

5. **Explain the founding seat the way Up explains early access.** (Pattern 5.) A short screen reached from the founding row's "What is this?" link. Three sentences: "500 lifters pay once and keep Pro for as long as Catchline exists." "You get every Pro feature added later." "When the seats are gone this option is removed." Then one visible mark of membership, in Locket's manner: a "FOUNDING 137/500" line on the You tab and, as an option, on the share video's end card. No timer. When seats run out the row stays for a week reading "Founding member · all 500 seats taken" and is then removed.

6. **Put the invite on the paywall, not only in settings.** (Pattern 4.) The secondary button in change 3 opens a sheet modelled on Life Reset: headline "GIVE A MONTH OF PRO", line "A lifter who uses your code gets a month of Pro. So do you.", the code in large type with "Tap to copy", a row "Share your tracked lift to Instagram Stories" that attaches the 9:16 video with the code on the end card, then Messages, WhatsApp, More. Footnote: "Your month starts when they track their first lift."

7. **State the plan and the allowance on the You tab.** (Pattern 2.) A fixed row at the top: "Free · 1 video a week · next: Mon 23 Nov" with "See Pro" at the right. For Pro: "Pro · yearly · renews 17 Nov 2027". For founding: "Founding member 137 of 500". ChatGPT and Ahead both give the reset time on a settings screen, so the rule is never a surprise.

8. **Restore top left, exit as text under the button, one maker's line.** (Patterns 6 and 7.) "Restore" as plain text in the top-left corner of the full paywall. "Not now" under the button. Above the plan block, two lines in body type: "Catchline is made by one lifter. Your video is tracked on your phone and never leaves it." This replaces star ratings and award badges, which Catchline does not have.

## What to avoid

- **Countdown clocks and "spots remaining" on a discount.** QUITTR shows "Lifetime Deal 80% off", "Once you close the offer, it's gone!" and "9 spots remaining" (https://mobbin.com/screens/e788be9f-e751-45ef-a247-420c703c6982). FocusFlight shows a ticket with "-58%" and "Expires In 59:52" (https://mobbin.com/screens/5a020989-c20a-4f45-b35c-b7f92a703f1d). Placify runs a "23:10:37" clock over a struck-through price (https://mobbin.com/screens/3866cef7-dea3-4979-bb93-edab028f59ec). Catchline's 500 seats are a real limit. Putting a clock or a fake "9 left" next to it would make the true number look like the same trick.
- **A limit message with no time in it.** X's "You have reached the usage limit now, please come back later." (https://mobbin.com/screens/ec331ba6-f66b-4870-8299-079aba04b269) gives no day and no count. For a weekly allowance that reads as "the app is broken".
- **A trial timeline.** The "Today, In 2 Days, In 3 Days" rail (5 Minute Journal, https://mobbin.com/screens/0b03c06a-63c8-43dd-87ed-58fecc57f0a1) and the "activate free trial" toggle (pushr, https://mobbin.com/screens/107957d2-0a20-46d2-b12b-65414f7809bc) explain a charge that follows a card-on-file trial. Catchline's trial is the free weekly video, with no card. Adding a trial rail would add a promise to explain and a cancellation to fear, at a $2.99 price where neither helps.
- **Feature-tick lists and loss-framed exits.** Six ticks of generic features (Hevy, Ladder) fit apps with many features. Catchline's Pro is one thing, more videos, and the list would be padding. Flo's exit link "choose limited access without full Flo benefits" is the wording to stay away from.
