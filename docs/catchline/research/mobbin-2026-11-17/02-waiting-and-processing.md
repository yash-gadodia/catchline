# 02. Waiting and processing

Mobbin study, 17 November 2026, iOS only. Adds to the October notes (study 27, section 1.5), which already cover Calm Sleep's checklist, CapCut translate, IKEA Kreativ at 8 minutes, Beside, Lapse and the Cal AI home row.

**The most useful thing:** the best waits show the person's own material being worked on and write what has been found so far. CapCut prints a running description of the footage beside the percentage; Noom shows the heart rate while the face scan is still running.
Catchline can do this honestly, because the plate and the joints are found frame by frame: draw them on the lifter's clip as they are found, so the minute of waiting is the first half of the reveal.

## Patterns

### 1. The wait shows what has been found so far, on the person's own material

The screen is not a spinner over a blank. It shows the input and writes or draws partial findings as they arrive.

- CapCut, auto-clip: "Creating clips... 85.95%", then "Hang tight while we analyze your footage to find the highlights." and a paragraph describing the footage as it is read ("The footage captures a car smoothly navigating a wet, winding road under a cloudy sky..."). Footer: "This may take a while. We'll notify you when it's ready!" and a "Minimize" link. https://mobbin.com/screens/c24f8420-c9ea-4ddd-9d6a-5a623b31bdcc
- Noom, face scan: the face sits in an oval with a ring filling round it and a counter on the ring (13). Under it a "Lighting quality" row of five suns, and a card that already shows "Heart rate 98 bpm" while the scan continues. https://mobbin.com/screens/963e2be4-7ba0-43f5-9afa-89292d87ef33
- Lemonade Insurance: "One sec while I analyze some data and calculate your price..." Four lines tick off, then a running total appears on the same screen: "Total savings per year -$17". Button "See my quote". https://mobbin.com/screens/015cebca-a6c2-48be-bb6a-1095a6df5cf5
- GoPro Quik: "Analyzing your footage..." is drawn over the person's own video, which stays visible and scrubbable under it. https://mobbin.com/screens/9e549555-1c9e-4bdf-b131-7908c7cc7142

Why it works: a partial result is proof that the work is real and is about this clip. Noom's early heart rate also means the person has something to read before the scan ends.

### 2. Named steps where the current step has its own sub-line

Three or four steps in the app's own vocabulary. Done steps are ticked, the current one carries a second line saying what is happening now, the rest are greyed.

- Brainly: "Generating your notes / This may take a moment". Four rows: "Reading the conversation" (ticked), "Identifying key concepts" (ticked), "Structuring your notes" with the sub-line "Ordering topics so they flow naturally...", "Final touches" (grey). https://mobbin.com/screens/5dcf2f0b-0988-470d-b180-52b26ec69833
- Wabi: a card with the title of the thing being made, "Estimated time 8min 2s", a percentage, and four steps ("Creating plan / Processing...", "Developing logic", "Designing screens", "Final touches"). https://mobbin.com/screens/74077ac9-bcc0-481b-88f1-0ddea0c52d11
- Lovi, skin scan: two stacked bars on black, "Analysing scan results..." (full, ticked) and "Building a scan report..." (filling). Nothing else on the screen. https://mobbin.com/screens/d87832ec-d479-4d9a-ab7c-36cee99c6005
- Ladder, food log: the typed meal stays on screen and three ticked lines repeat the person's own words back ("Looking up sourdough bread, avocado, and soft boiled egg"). https://mobbin.com/screens/773d535d-108a-45c4-b177-86925d6a7b24

Why it works: the sub-line on the current step is the part that moves, so the screen never looks stuck. Lovi shows that two steps are enough when the two are honestly different jobs.

### 3. A unit the person can check, not only a percentage

Progress is given in the natural unit of the work.

- [untitled], video export: "Exporting video..." with "0:29 / 1:11" inside the preview and "899/900 frames rendered - 0s remaining" under the bar, in monospace. https://mobbin.com/screens/3ce0c2c6-33e0-4c3c-9f1c-6358fb337929
- TikTok Tako: a card in the chat, "Generating video... 5 mins left", sub-line "Analyzing your prompt", thumbnail of the source image, and a bar. https://mobbin.com/screens/a8071f22-df16-4c2f-88e3-4f372b430105
- IKEA Kreativ, a shorter case than the 8-minute one in study 27: "Estimated processing time / 1 Minute", "Room uploaded" ticked, "Analyzing & preparing room" in progress. https://mobbin.com/screens/d90e1d51-b03d-48cc-8105-5a258c0a3c22
- Google Photos, photo to video: "Polishing pixels...", "This may take about a minute.", "16%". https://mobbin.com/screens/c99ceb78-91cc-43d3-a055-352407f25919

Why it works: "899/900 frames" can only be true. A bare percentage on a one-minute job is often a guess, and people know it. A time estimate stated up front ("about a minute") sets the contract before impatience starts.

### 4. Permission to leave is the headline, not the footnote

When the job is long enough to lose people, the screen says first that they may go.

- Character AI: headline "You can close this while it's generating", sub-line "Once completed, you'll receive a notification to view your video creation". The status ("Queued for generation...") is small, over a blurred preview. https://mobbin.com/screens/b20bd436-818d-4460-8990-8e3cd7cb1e59
- TikTok Tako: "This might take a few minutes, so feel free to keep chatting or leave Tako. I'll message you when it's ready." Then the cost, stated plainly: "1 of 2 video generations used today." https://mobbin.com/screens/a8071f22-df16-4c2f-88e3-4f372b430105
- Meta AI: a tile with a ring at 51% and one line under it, "You'll be notified when your vibe is ready." https://mobbin.com/screens/ec848afc-743d-4488-8937-7a8b74a51144
- ElevenLabs: a banner, "Generation started. Your creation will appear in the Home and Creator tabs." It says where to find the result, not only that it will come. https://mobbin.com/screens/c50ff3eb-c798-4f14-8a5b-c098f4828f81

Why it works: it removes the fear that leaving cancels the job. ElevenLabs adds the missing half: where the result will be.

### 5. The item exists in its list while it is being worked on

The thing being processed already has a row in the place where it will live, with its status in the row.

- WHOOP, lab results: the list row reads "External Lab (0) / Analyzing your upload" with the date, and a card under it explains: "Extracting biomarkers from your uploaded lab results and matching to WHOOP Advanced Labs biomarkers. This can take several minutes. We'll notify you when it is ready to view." https://mobbin.com/screens/d68d017b-c7ec-4c1e-be9d-65d4cdefee86
- IKEA Kreativ: the scan is a card in "My scans & designs" with its two stages, and "Capture again" sits right under it, so a second one can be started during the wait. https://mobbin.com/screens/d90e1d51-b03d-48cc-8105-5a258c0a3c22
- Alta: three uploaded items each show their own spinner on their own tile, with "Add All" waiting at the top. https://mobbin.com/screens/ac7935a2-9097-490f-b2c0-07a830d1cd73

Why it works: backing out of the processing screen lands somewhere that still shows the job. WHOOP's row says what is being done in one clause, which is more than "Processing".

### 6. The preview resolves out of blur

The space where the result will appear is occupied from the start by a blurred or dotted version of the input, with the percentage in a corner.

- Grok, image to video: the source photo under a grid of dots and heavy blur, "07%" bottom left, the prompt and "480p · 6s" under it. The share and save buttons are already present but inactive. https://mobbin.com/screens/1ea8ed41-ceab-4d98-89c9-24691e4ba3e9
- TikTok, AI Create in the editor: a hexagon with "7%" and "Generating..." over the clip, a "Processing edits..." line, and the timeline segment itself labelled "AI generating...". The editor stays usable. https://mobbin.com/screens/eab224f1-efe4-4b74-a834-1ac257729aba
- Polarsteps: "Generating your awesome Trip Reel" over a blurred photo from the person's own trip. https://mobbin.com/screens/82fc6f83-a013-45e5-8a61-5dbb8f966e62

Why it works: the layout does not jump when the result arrives, and the person is already looking at the right place.

### 7. One fact while waiting, placed above the progress bar

A single line of teaching, with the progress and the current step still visible under it.

- Noom: "Did you know... People who do strength training twice a week have a 10-20% lower risk of dying from any cause." with the source cited, and under it a bar and "CREATING A VISUAL BASELINE FOR YOUR HEALTH...". https://mobbin.com/screens/9ed8127a-1947-4028-89d0-f866478dc1d1
- GoFundMe: "This may take a few moments." over a skeleton of the page being written, and a white card: "Did you know? Fundraisers shared on the first day are 3x more likely to receive donations." The fact is about the next action. https://mobbin.com/screens/dcc555fc-4e3d-4bce-a4e6-36ce91de6dc5
- stoic.: "WHILE YOU'RE WAITING / Would you like to read a few amazing facts about yourself?" with a "Yes" button. The reading is opt-in. https://mobbin.com/screens/0bdc5da1-539f-4f51-afc2-3b17e76ea753
- MyDyson: "This will just take a moment. While we connect, you can learn how the MyDyson app complements your machine." with a pager of cards. https://mobbin.com/screens/b2f3205c-9ecc-4ce5-bc29-f926e4880c34

Why it works: GoFundMe's fact prepares the step that follows the wait. That is the model to copy: teach how to read the result that is about to appear.

## What Catchline should do

Ordered by expected effect on the first lift.

1. **Draw the track on the lifter's clip as it is found** (patterns 1 and 6). The processing screen is the clip, full height, paused on the first frame and dimmed. As frames are tracked, the plate ring appears and the bar path extends in red; the joint lines follow when the pose pass runs. Nothing is invented: only frames already tracked are drawn. If live drawing is too costly in the first build, show the ring on the first frame where the plate is found, with the line "Plate found", as Noom shows the heart rate early.
2. **Three named steps with a moving sub-line, in the frame unit** (patterns 2 and 3). Under the clip:
   - "1 PREPARING THE CLIP" (ticked when done)
   - "2 FINDING THE PLATE" with sub-line "frame 212 of 540"
   - "3 FINDING THE BODY" with sub-line "frame 0 of 540"
   Above the steps, once, "About a minute. It runs on this phone." Monospace for the frame counts, as [untitled] does.
3. **Say early that the plate was found or was not** (pattern 1). After the first second of plate tracking, either "Plate found" or "No plate found yet. Is the whole plate in frame?" with "Keep going" and "Pick another clip". This turns the quarter of finished tracks with no bar path into a decision at second ten instead of a disappointment at second sixty.
4. **The lift is a row in Lifts from the moment Track is pressed** (pattern 5). Row copy: "Clean · Finding the plate · 39%". Leaving the processing screen returns to this row. Tapping it reopens the live view.
5. **Tell the truth about leaving** (pattern 4). Tracking runs on the phone, so the Character AI promise ("you can close this") may not hold when the app is suspended. Copy if it can continue for a short while in the background: "You can put the phone down. Keep Catchline open." Copy for the push permission, asked only here: "Notify me when it is tracked", and the push itself: "Your clean is tracked. See the bar path."
6. **One line that teaches how to read the result** (pattern 7). Above the steps, rotating every ten seconds, three lines at most: "The red line is the centre of the plate.", "A straight line close to the body is the usual aim.", "Speed is how fast the bar rises, in metres per second." No trivia, no testimonials.
7. **Keep the action buttons in place but inactive** (pattern 6). "SHARE" and "SAVE" sit under the clip greyed out during the wait, as in Grok, so the reveal changes the picture and nothing else moves.

## What to avoid

- **"Please don't leave this screen."** WHOOP's video render says "This might take several minutes. Please don't leave this screen until the rendering process is complete." (https://mobbin.com/screens/e7c9bc50-3282-43b5-b047-46a1f083eea5). If Catchline has the same technical limit, it should still be said as what happens ("Tracking pauses if you leave. It picks up when you come back.") and the job must resume, not restart.
- **Jokes and fake steps.** Lemonade's fourth step is "Finding the best coffee spot" and Discord's loader says "Detecting organic lifeforms...". It is charming once on a five-second wait. On a one-minute wait about a lifter's own technique it reads as padding, and a fake step makes the real ones doubtful.
- **Promotion in the wait.** Any Distance uses its loading screen for "Tag @anydistance to get featured!" and social icons; Evernote shows a star rating and a quote under its progress ring. The lifter has not seen a result yet. Asking for a tag or showing a testimonial before value is delivered spends trust.
- **A spinner over a dimmed form.** Once shows "Creating your special film" over the greyed-out previous screen. It gives no sense of length or of what is being done.

Screens (iOS):
- "analysing your video progress screen with named steps and percentage" (deep, 16)
- "AI generation in progress screen saying you can leave the app and we will notify you when it is ready" (deep, 16)
- "health or fitness app scan measuring in progress with live preview and countdown" (standard, 14). Mostly workout timers and Live Activities; only the Noom face scan was on topic.
- "loading screen that shows a tip or did you know fact while the result is being prepared" (deep, 14)
- "list with an item still processing showing progress in the row, and a notification that your result is ready" (standard, 14). No "your result is ready" notification screens came back; that half of the query found nothing.
- "photo being scanned with animated scan line and detected items appearing one by one" (standard, 14). Mostly empty barcode viewfinders. Yazio, Jomo and BitePal show a scan line over the photo; none shows items appearing one by one in a still.

Not found: a sports or movement analysis app's processing screen. No screen showed a skeleton or path being drawn during analysis. The recommendation to draw the track live comes from CapCut, Noom and Grok by analogy, not from a direct example.
