## The three findings that change the plan

1. **There is no real incumbent on iPhone.** The category leader's installs are on Android; on the US App Store the
   top results for "bar path" have 46, 0, 5 and 0 ratings, and two older apps have left the stores.
2. **First by users worldwide needs Android.** Android is about 72% of phones worldwide and 75 to 90% in most of the
   traditional weightlifting countries. A reduced Android version is scoped at 22 to 30 working days (09-android.md).
3. **Consumer subscriptions alone do not reach the revenue goal.** The addressable base of iPhone lifters who film is
   roughly 0.3 to 0.6 million; a coach tier is the lever (04, 11).

The ranked plan that follows from these is in [00-plan.md](00-plan.md).
