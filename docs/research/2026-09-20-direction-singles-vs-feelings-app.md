# Direction check, 20 Sep 2026: widen to singles, or pivot to a feelings app?

Question from Yash: "couples is a bad idea, what about singles? post-fight is just one use case." Sources: Mobbin (How We Feel, stoic, ABY Journal, Finch, Bloom, Liven, Ahead, 5 Minute Journal, Wysa, Copilot), docs/PRD-solo.md §1, the 9 Sep research verdict, the 16 Sep decision ledger.

## What the evidence says
1. The 9 Sep verdict already settled the "who": the ICP is a moment ("just had a fight, don't know what to say"), not a demographic. "Couples" is a leftover v1 label, not the product.
2. The defensible part (PRD §1) is the right place to be at 11pm + lowering the shame of asking. Both survive a person-agnostic framing unchanged. Neither survives a general feelings app, which has no moment.
3. Mobbin: the feelings-processing category converges on emotion pickers, daily reminders, streaks, mood charts, "patterns" dashboards and a mascot. Every one of those is something Parallax's product laws or the v2 cut removed on purpose. ABY Journal's "ABY noticed…" card is the nearest thing to a memory-informed read, and it is affirmation, not a concession.
4. Nothing on Mobbin ends in a sendable sentence that names what the other person is not wrong about. The gap is unchanged.
5. The 9 Sep research killed the memory archive (a searchable fight log is a liability object). A patterns page reopens that.

## Recommendation
Widen the who, keep the what. "What to say after a fight" with anyone: partner, friend, parent, boss, flatmate. This is mostly copy (partner → them), store category/keywords, and the read prompt. Do not pivot to a feelings app: that is the daily-loop v1 shape again, in a crowded field, with no moment.

Over time: ship the memory-informed line inside the result first. The Over time page stays a plain list of past reads plus one paragraph; no per-theme counts, no dashboard, until the 3-week test says people come back.

Run the 3-week test with two single friends who had a fight with anyone in the last month, alongside Dani.
