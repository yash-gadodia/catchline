Parallax — design brief. ONE screen: Capture. Plus one result variant.

Do not design the other screens. Working/Retry, Result, Safety and Settings were all settled across earlier rounds (v6/v7) and are being built as designed. Adding to them now costs more than it earns.

WHAT THE APP IS NOW

One person, alone. No partner account, no invite, no link, no shared view — ever. The partner is the subject of the writing, never a user.

Something goes wrong between you and them. You log it. The app returns three things: what is underneath it for you, the specific thing your partner is probably not wrong about, and one sentence under 40 words you could actually send. You edit that sentence, then copy it. Nothing else leaves the app.

WHY CAPTURE IS THE ONLY SCREEN WORTH DESIGNING

The research concluded there is no defensible product opportunity here — the interpretation is commodity, and a saved ChatGPT prompt plus a note reproduces most of it. Two things it cannot reproduce, and both are this screen:

1. Being the right place to be at 11pm. Opening a general chatbot means arriving at a blank cursor in the same app you used for a work bug, composing a paragraph at the moment of lowest composure. Time-to-output under duress is a real axis and it is entirely UI.

2. Lowering the shame of asking. There is a felt difference between "I am a person who types my partner's behaviour into ChatGPT" and "I opened the thing that is for this." A purpose-built surface grants permission.

So judge every choice on this screen against one question: can someone who has just been snapped at get from launch to submitted in under 30 seconds, without composing prose?

THE SCREEN

It is the launch screen. No onboarding, no account, no tour, no splash. Keyboard raised on open.

- A large free-text box, PRIMARY. Prose is what the model needs — the concession cannot be specific without it. Do not demote the box to a collapsed row.
- A MIC button, prominent enough to read as the obvious path rather than an alternative. Speech is roughly three times faster than thumb-typing and survives being upset. No app in this category opens on the mic; this is the main unexplored move.
- SENTENCE STEMS on the keyboard accessory bar, not chips above the box. Tapping one appends it and hands over the first four words: "She said…", "I let it go when…", "The second time I…", "I felt like the one who…". They are openers, not tags or categories. Reference: Bloom's entry screen does exactly this.
- An inline "+" in the stem row that becomes a text field in place, for the thing that is not in the list. No modal.
- Submit live from the first keystroke or stem. The primary button never greys out once there is anything at all.
- A second, NAMED button: "Just save". It writes locally and makes no model call. Naming it is the point — it is permission to log something without being analysed, and it must not be an implicit trick where submit happens to work with no text.
- One quiet line about where the words go. Honest, not reassuring: the app is not privileged the way a doctor or a lawyer is.

REGISTER

Someone upset at 11pm. Nothing jumpy, nothing celebratory, no illustration, no mascot. The best line found in this whole category is ABY Journal's "No perfect words needed. Just what's true right now" — that is the tone, not the wording.

Warm and quiet. Serif display against a clean UI sans, soft ground, generous whitespace, minimal chrome. Match the existing app. Note that iOS draws continuous corners: rounded surfaces should read as squircles, not circular arcs.

ALSO DESIGN: THE NO-BRIDGE RESULT

The trigger was widened from fights to friction — flat tones, the thing you let go. For a large share of those, the correct output is deflationary and there is nothing to send.

"There is no bridge here" is a first-class output, not an error or an empty state. It names what it noticed, says plainly that the honest thing is to let this one go, and offers nothing to copy. It must feel like a complete and correct answer, not like the app failed to produce the main thing. This is the one genuinely new output state and I want to see it.

SHOW ME

- Capture in at least two directions so I can compare.
- Capture in three states: empty on launch, mid-dictation, and with text plus a stem tapped.
- The no-bridge result.

DO NOT DESIGN

A daily prompt, pulse, reminder or streak. A browsable archive or list of past fights. Any partner surface, invite or share. A widget. Scores, ratings or percentages. A content library. Onboarding. Credibility theatre — cited studies, clinician logos, "clinically backed".

Two rounds maximum. There is a four-evening build waiting and a written stop condition; the risk now is designing instead of testing.
