Three things grew back that were deliberately cut, and the paywall needs rethinking.

1. REMOVE THE STREAK.
Home shows "{n} in a row ended with a sentence or a step" once paired. Streaks were removed from this product wholesale, and this is a score on the relationship, which the product's own rule forbids. It also changes what the app is for: the moment there is a run to protect, the user is optimising the run instead of handling the fight. Delete the streak state and the chip entirely. No replacement, no softer version, no "gentle" counter.

2. DECIDE WHETHER THE RECORD EARNS ITS PLACE.
Record currently lists every fight by title, date, topic and what it touched — "Dinner at your mum's, again", "S$400 on the console". A scrollable list of grievance titles is a liability object inside a relationship: it turns "I apologised for that" into "you did this in March", and it is the first thing someone reaches for when angry. The 30-day raw deletion helps and should stay, but the titles are the problem, not the raw text.

Preferred: cut the list. Keep only the decaying pattern line ("Plans, three times. Each time it touched the same thing: my weekend is the one that moves") and the entry point to write. That was the original design and it was right.

If the list stays, it must not be browsable as a history: no titles that name the fight, no status labels, no scroll. Show at most the pattern plus the current night.

3. CUT GUIDES, OR CUT WHAT MAKES IT GENERIC.
A library of articles is the thing users pan competitors for. A real review of a comparable app: "The course content has nothing new or innovative. You can find this information practically free on the internet." Also remove "reviewed by SAC-registered counsellors" and the quotes from other couples — both are credibility theatre, and this product does not need borrowed authority.

If something like Guides survives, it should be one short piece of reading offered inside a result, chosen by what this fight was about, and never a tab.

4. THE PAYWALL IS UPSIDE DOWN.
"Tonight is always free" is exactly the right law — writing, the two things back and the sentence must never be paywalled. But the four Plus features are: the record past 90 days, patterns across fights, every guide, unlimited trade views. Three of those sell things this feedback is asking you to cut or shrink. The free tier is the good product and Plus is charging for the accumulation around it.

Rework it as one of these, not a tweak of the current list:
(a) No subscription at all. Revenue is not the point of this product, and no paywall is more honest than a paywall around the weak half.
(b) A single one-time price for the app, with everything included and no tiers.
(c) If there must be a subscription, the only candidate worth charging for is the partner side — the invite, trading views, and agreeing one next step — because it is the only part with real ongoing cost and real added value. Charge for that alone and drop the other three.

Show me (a) and (c) as separate screens so I can compare.

5. MISSING SCREENS. None of these exist yet and all are needed.
- Profile / settings. There is no account screen anywhere.
- Restore Purchases. Apple requires it and the shipped app already has one.
- Manage or cancel subscription.
- Delete my account and everything I wrote. One tap, really deletes. This is a safety requirement, not a settings nicety.
- Notification permission ask, with a plain reason and no content ever shown in a preview.
- Marcus's view. The invite promises "he can read it before installing anything" and that web page is designed nowhere. It is the first thing a partner ever sees of this product.

The tab bar is Home / Record / Guides, so there is currently no home for any of it. If Record and Guides both shrink or go, the tabs are free for something that matters.
