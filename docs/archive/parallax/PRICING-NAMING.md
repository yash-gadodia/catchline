# Pricing & naming — research verdict (2026-09-16)

## Pricing call

CHANGE — but not yet. Keep the approved ladder (S$6.98 / S$59.98 / US$79.99) untouched through the 3-week self-test: testers never hit the paywall (the read is free, 1/day), and pre-PMF the numbers are irrelevant. The day the app is offered to anyone beyond friends, flip the ladder in App Store Connect — pure config, no binary, no review, approval not at risk (Apple's own docs: subscription price changes need "no app review or new app version"; with ~zero subscribers the consent machinery never triggers).

Exact new ladder:
- Monthly: US$6.99 / S$9.98 (up from US$4.99 / S$6.98). This is the last Apple tier under the psychological S$10 line in Singapore, still undercuts every AI-priced SG comparable (Intellect S$8.98, Rosebud/Calm S$19.98, Ash US$19.99), and moves break-even from 8.6 to 5.7 months — exactly the 12-app category median and inside the AI-cohort norm (7.0). Do NOT jump to US$9.99: it maps to S$14.98, an 18%-over-spot tier that doubles the SG sticker and abandons Singapore-first.
- Annual: UNCHANGED at US$39.99 / S$59.98 — it already sits above the US$34.80 category median; cutting it would fix the ratio by destroying the LTV product (annual retains 44.1% at 12 months vs 17% monthly). Pre-select it on the paywall.
- Lifetime: pull US$79.99 from the paywall at the same moment and re-list at US$99.99 (2.5x annual) only if super-fan demand shows up. At 2.0x annual it is the cheapest lifetime multiple observed anywhere (only comparable: Stoic at 4.3–7.5x; HN consensus 3–4 years) and it undercuts your own subscription on a product with perpetual inference cost.

Mechanics: implement monthly as a NEW subscription SKU offered in place of the old one (zero notification machinery, old SKU keeps renewing for any grandfathered friends), or as a price increase with "keep current price for existing subscribers" checked — either way nothing rides a binary, and remember Apple does NOT auto-equalize subscription prices across storefronts: set the SG tier (S$9.98) explicitly.

One-sentence rationale to repeat: "Our 8.6-month break-even isn't an expensive annual, it's the cheapest monthly in the entire category (38% of the US$12.99 median) — so we raise monthly to the last tier under S$10 and leave the annual alone."

## Naming call

RENAME to "One Side" — ship it with the pivot binary, not after. "Parallax" fails every test that matters: the bare-name search is owned by 3D-wallpaper apps (your live app doesn't rank top-10 for its own name even in Singapore), a same-category doppelganger ("Parallax: For Couples", released a month before yours) holds temporal priority in the couples space, and the word literally means one thing seen from two viewpoints — a precise description of the product you just killed. "One Side" has an empty App Store namespace, matches the winning plain-phrase pattern of category leaders (How We Feel, I Am Sober), and is honest to the point of wit: the app only ever hears one side and knows it, which is exactly the frame that lets "the thing your partner is probably not wrong about" land.

What changes when (verified against Apple's platform-version reference — the "description is editable anytime" assumption is wrong, that rule ended in 2017):
- TODAY, no binary: promotional text only (170 chars). Set it to: "New: use it alone — talk or paste the fight, get back what's underneath and one sentence worth sending." This is the only pre-binary lever; use it to soft-pivot on top of the frozen couples listing.
- NEXT BINARY, all together: name → "One Side", subtitle, description, keywords (keep fight/argument/conflict/repair, add text/say/screenshot; drop couples), and screenshots showing the paste-the-WhatsApp-thread flow. There is no half-pivot — bundle everything into the ONE SIDE submission.
- BEFORE committing: run a real trademark clearance (IPOS first, Singapore-first product; the empty-namespace read is App Store only, not a mark search). If "One Side" dies there or in a vibe check, the fallback is "Unsaid" — equally empty namespace, names the "what's underneath" beat, accepting its heavier tone.

## Listing copy

Subtitle (25/30 chars): "What to say after a fight"
— claims verbatim the highest-intent query on the store ("what to text after a fight" returns zero relevant apps today), carries the search work the quiet name can't, and pre-empts the one misread of the name (it's about your side, not taking sides).

## Risks & reversals

PRICING RISKS: (1) S$9.98 monthly sits 2.5x above Between (S$3.98), the couples-app SG users actually know — mitigated because the pivot exits the couples frame into AI-personal-tool territory where SG anchors are S$18–20. (2) Raising price with zero demand signal could mask elasticity — mitigated by timing it at first-stranger, when the free 1-read/day tier still lets everyone try it. (3) Pulling lifetime forfeits upfront super-fan cash at exactly friends-and-family stage. REVERSAL: trivially cheap — all pricing is ASC config; re-add the old SKU or schedule a price decrease same-day, no binary, no review. One caution: an applied price *increase* can't be reversed, only superseded by a new change — the new-SKU route avoids even that. Separate flag: the proposed free tier's 1-read/day meter has zero precedent in 12 comparables (everyone gates breadth/memory, never the crisis moment by count) — it's the piece most likely to feel punitive at 11pm after a fight; consider gating history instead before it ships.

NAMING RISKS: (1) "One Side" is two generic words — near-unprotectable as a mark, so copycats can't be stopped; acceptable because findability beats defensibility for a founder-daily-first app. (2) IPOS/USPTO clearance not yet run — the empty-namespace check was App Store only; if it fails, fall back to "Unsaid". (3) "Taking sides" misread — the subtitle and first paragraph carry the corrective framing. (4) Exact-title #1 rank for "one side" is inferred from Apple's exact-match weighting, not guaranteed against generic-word noise. (5) You lose the (near-zero) existing ASO equity and any word-of-mouth pointing at "Parallax" — which currently routes half its searchers to a doppelganger anyway. REVERSAL: cheap but slow — a rename is just the next version submission on the same app record (v2 already proved a full pivot on one record survives review); the only true one-way door is time spent, not the record, ratings, or approval. LISTING RISK: whether review accepts couples→solo repositioning on the same record was not verifiable in advance — mitigate with clean review notes; precedent (your own v2.0.0 pivot approval) is on your side.

# Track positions

## PRICING COMPARABLES 2026
Parallax's real pricing problem is inverted from the brief: against 12 live comparables (US App Store IAP lists, fetched 2026-09-16) the median monthly is $12.99 and the median annual/monthly break-even is 5.7 months — not 2–5 — and the AI-inference cohort Parallax actually belongs to (Rosebud 8.3, Ash 8.5, Finch 7.0, Sonia 6.5) clusters at 7–8.5 months, so the 8.6-month ratio is normal for AI apps; what is abnormal is the US$4.99 monthly, the cheapest in the entire set at 38% of median and a quarter of the AI-mental-health floor ($19.99 Ash). The comparable-implied move is to raise monthly to $9.99–12.99 (break-even falls to 3.3–4.0 months at the existing approved $39.99 annual, no new App Store review risk on the annual SKU), keep annual where it is, and either kill or reprice the $79.99 lifetime — the only comparable selling lifetime (Stoic, $299) prices it at 4.3–7.5x annual, versus Parallax's 2.0x which undercuts its own subscription. The free-tier plan is directionally right (every comparable gates breadth/memory/features, never the emotional moment) but the 1-read/day meter has no precedent in the set and is the piece most likely to feel punitive after a fight.

## PRICING STRATEGY EVIDENCE
Keep the approved tiers live through the friends-and-family phase — pricing is near-irrelevant pre-PMF (Andreessen/Vohra consensus), the free read means testers never touch the paywall, and the approved app needs zero pricing work to serve the audience ladder — but fix the broken rung the moment a stranger can find the app, because the fix is pure App Store Connect config (no review, no new binary, and with ~zero subscribers no consent machinery). The data says the 8.6-month break-even is caused by monthly being underpriced (US$4.99 vs a $10 category median), not annual being overpriced (US$39.99 is already above the $34.80 median), and monthly's real job is anchor, not product: yearly plans retain 44.1% at 12 months vs 17% for monthly, and yearly-dominant apps earn ~2x RPI. Exact ladder at revamp: monthly → US$8.99 (≈S$12.98), keep annual at US$39.99/S$59.98 → break-even falls to ~4.4 months, inside the 2-5 comparable band, with annual pre-selected on the paywall; lifetime → US$99.99 (2.5x annual — current US$79.99 is 2.0x, bottom of the 2-12x range and below HN's 3-4yr consensus, dangerous for a product with perpetual inference cost); implement the monthly change either as a price increase with "keep current price for existing subscribers" checked, or cleaner, as a new SKU while the old one is simply removed from the offering — either way the approved app, the review status, and any grandfathered friends are untouched. Do not add a timed free trial; the 1-read/day free tier is the trial, and it fits the episodic value event better than any 3-day clock.

## SINGAPORE PRICING
S$6.98/mo reads normal-to-cheap in Singapore for a personal-tool app — it undercuts the homegrown wellness incumbent Intellect (S$8.98/mo), is a third of what Paired, Calm, Headspace and Rosebud charge on the SG storefront (S$18–22/mo), and is only S$3 above Between, the low-anchor Asian couples app most SG users actually know. The SGD tier structure is the real strategic constraint: US$4.99 maps to S$6.98 (essentially FX + 9% GST, netting ~US$4.28/mo after GST and the 15% small-business cut), US$6.99 maps to S$9.98 (the last tier under the psychological S$10 line), and US$9.99 jumps to S$14.98. So the cleanest SG-aware fix for the 8.6-month break-even is to raise monthly to US$6.99/S$9.98 — still cheaper than every AI-priced comparable in SG — rather than cut the annual, and note that Apple does NOT auto-equalize existing subscription prices across storefronts, so the SG tiers must be changed explicitly in App Store Connect.

## NAMING
Rename — "Parallax" should not survive the pivot. It fails on all three tests: the bare-name search is owned by 3D-wallpaper apps and the live app doesn't rank top-10 for its own name even in Singapore; a same-category doppelganger ("Parallax: For Couples", released a month earlier) creates permanent confusion with temporal priority against you; and the word's meaning — one thing seen from two viewpoints — is a precise description of the product being killed, not the one being built. Rename to "One Side": the namespace is empty on the App Store, it matches the winning plain-phrase pattern of category leaders (How We Feel, I Am Sober), and it is honest to the point of wit — the app only ever hears one side, and knows it, which is exactly the frame that makes beat (b) ("the thing your partner is probably not wrong about") land without therapy-coding. Its two real costs — a possible "taking sides" misread and weak trademark protectability of a generic phrase — are manageable: the subtitle ("after the fight" or similar) must carry both the search keywords and the corrective framing, and defensibility for a founder-daily-first app matters less than findability. Fallback if "One Side" dies in a proper trademark search or a vibe check with real users: "Unsaid" — equally empty namespace, one-word register, names the (a) beat — accepting its slightly heavier, more melancholic tone. Since the pivot is a new product on the same App Store record anyway (v2 already did this once), the rename should ship with the pivot, not after.

## ASO + LISTING
Rewrite the whole listing around the empty query, and ship it all in one binary. The data says the solo-conflict niche is real but embryonic — a dozen AI argument-judge/mediator apps launched in the last 18 months, none past 32 ratings — while the single highest-intent phrase, "what to text after a fight", returns literally nothing relevant on the App Store today. Meanwhile the live listing ("Parallax: Couples" / "both sides. one middle ground." / "when you two fight") tells a solo user this app is not for them in the first three words. Since Apple version-locks name, subtitle, description, keywords AND screenshots (only promotional text is editable anytime — the task brief's "description changes anytime" is outdated, verified against Apple's own reference), there is no half-pivot available: bundle name → "Parallax: After the Fight" (or at minimum drop ":Couples"), subtitle → "What to say after a fight" (25 chars), the solo-first description above, and screenshots showing the paste-the-WhatsApp-thread flow into the ONE SIDE binary submission, and bridge the gap immediately with promotional text ("New: use it alone — paste the fight, get one sentence worth sending"). Keep keywords fight/argument/conflict/repair, add text/say/screenshot; skip "couples"/"boyfriend" terms — those SERPs belong to Paired-scale incumbents and companion-bot spam respectively.
