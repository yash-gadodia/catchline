# v2.0.0 submission — Parallax: Couples

What changed, and what App Review needs to know. Companion to `docs/SUBMISSION.md`
(v1.0, approved 14-07-2026); the app record, bundle id and IAPs are unchanged.

## What v2.0 is

v1 was a daily three-question game with AI conflict mediation buried in a tab.
v2 inverts that: **the mediator is the product**. Each partner privately writes
their side of a disagreement; neither ever sees the other's raw words; the app
returns one shared synthesis (common ground, what's underneath for each of them,
one kind thing each could say) and remembers what worked for next time.

The daily game is gone. What remains is Home (a one-tap daily pulse plus the way
in), Refocus (the mediation flow), and Memory (the couple's record).

## Review notes (paste into App Review Information)

Parallax helps a couple work through a disagreement. Each partner privately
writes their own side; the app returns a shared, neutral summary. Neither
partner ever sees the other's raw text.

DEMO ACCOUNT: the supplied login is one half of a pre-paired couple with
history already seeded, so the full flow is reachable on a single device
without a second phone. Home → "Untangle something" walks the whole feature.

AI SAFETY. All AI output is generated by Anthropic's Claude and passes a
safety screening pass before any mediation or reflection is produced:
- Two-sided mediation FAILS CLOSED: if screening is unavailable the session
  does not proceed.
- Crisis and abuse signals bypass the model entirely and render static,
  human-written copy pointing to local helplines (SOS 1767, IMH, and
  findahelpline.com). That copy is server-side and never model-generated.
- A disagreement showing signs of abuse is never routed into "find the middle
  ground" mediation.
- Every AI result carries an on-screen AI disclosure and a note that this is
  not therapy. The app makes no medical, clinical or therapeutic claims.
- A gentle therapist signpost appears for couples using mediation repeatedly.

USER CONTENT. The text each partner writes is shown to exactly one known
person (their paired partner) and only ever as a neutral synthesis, never
verbatim. There is no public feed, no discovery, no stranger contact, and no
user-to-user messaging.

PURCHASES. Products are unchanged from the approved v1.0 set. The mediation
flow itself is free and never interrupted by a paywall; the subscription sells
memory features (the mediator reading the couple's own history, and their
patterns). One purchase covers both partners.
