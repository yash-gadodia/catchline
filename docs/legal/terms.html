The service

Catchline tracks a video of your lift on your iPhone. It follows the barbell plate (or your hips, for a hanging movement) and your body's joints on every frame, and draws the bar path and positions back on your own video. The tracking runs entirely on your phone. You can name, tag and add notes to your lifts, share a video you made, and export your data. We may change or withdraw features, and we will say so in the app when we do.

The tracking is an estimate

The bar path, positions, angles and any numbers the app shows are produced automatically from your video. They are estimates, not measurements from a sensor. Catchline is not coaching and not medical advice. It is not a coach, a physiotherapist or a doctor, it does not diagnose anything, and it does not prevent injury. It can be wrong, especially when the angle is off, the plate is hidden or the lighting is poor. You decide what to do with what it shows. If something hurts, stop and see a professional. You lift at your own risk.
