The service

Catchline takes a short side-on video of one weightlifting or gymnastics rep, measures body and bar positions from it, and returns one technique cue with the measurement behind it. Some plans also remember past cues and tell you whether a cue stuck, let you send a lift to a coach, and export your data. We may change or withdraw features, and we will say so in the app when we do.

The cue is an AI estimate

Every cue is produced automatically from video measurements and rule tables. It is labelled "AI estimate" because that is what it is. Catchline is a coaching aid, not a coach, a physiotherapist or a doctor. It does not diagnose anything, it does not prevent injury, and it can be wrong, especially when the angle is off, the bar is hidden or the lighting is poor. You decide what to do with a cue. If something hurts, stop and see a professional. You lift at your own risk.
